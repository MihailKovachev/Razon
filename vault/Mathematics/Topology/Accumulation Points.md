# Accumulation Points

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Accumulation Point
>
>Let $X$ be a [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) and let $S$ be a [subset](../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) of $X$.
>
>We say that $p \in X$ is an **accumulation point** / **limit point** / **cluster point** of $S$ if every [neighborhood](./Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) $N$ of $p$ contains at least one point of $S$ which is different from $p$:
>
>$$\exists p' \in S \text{ with } p' \in N \text{ and } p' \ne p$$
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Closedness and Accumulation Points
>
>Let $(X, \tau)$ be a [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md).
>
>A [subset](../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $S \subseteq X$ is [closed](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md#Closed%20Sets) if and only if it contains all of its [accumulation points](./Accumulation%20Points.md).
