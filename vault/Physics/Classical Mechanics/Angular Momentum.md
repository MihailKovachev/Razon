# Angular Momentum

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Angular Momentum
>
>Let $\mathcal{R}(O)$ be a [reference frame](./Reference%20Frames.md), let $P$ be a point and let $m$ be a [point mass](./Point%20Mass.md) with [momentum](./Momentum.md) $\boldsymbol{p}$.
>
>The **angular momentum** of $m$ with respect to $P$ is the [cross product](../../Mathematics/Algebra/Linear%20Algebra/Real%20Vectors/Cross%20Product.md) of the [distance vector](../Kinematics/Distance.md) $\boldsymbol{r}$ from $P$ to $m$ and $m$'s [momentum](./Momentum.md):
>
>$$\boldsymbol{r} \times \boldsymbol{p}$$
>

>
