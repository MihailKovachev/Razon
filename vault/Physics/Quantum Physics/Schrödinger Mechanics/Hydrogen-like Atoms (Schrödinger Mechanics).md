# Hydrogen-like Atoms (Schrödinger Mechanics)

A **hydrogen-like atom** is a system consisting of a single [electron](TODO) and a [nucleus](TODO) with one or more [protons](TODO). Such systems can be modeled very accurately in the framework of [Schrödinger mechanics](./Schrödinger%20Mechanics.md) because we can find closed-form solutions to their [Schrödinger equation](./Schrödinger%20Equation.md).
