# The General Linear Group

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: The General Linear Group
>
>Let $(V, F, +, \cdot)$ be a [vector space](../../../Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Vector%20Spaces.md).

>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: The General Linear Group
>>
>>This [group](../../../Algebra/Groups/Groups.md) is known as the **general linear group** of $V$.
