# Spherical Coordinate Representations (Real Scalar Fields)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Spherical Coordinate Representation
>
>Let $f: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}$ be a [real scalar field](./Real-Valued%20Functions%20of%20Multiple%20Real%20Variables.md) and let $\mathcal{T}: (0,+\infty) \times (0,\uppi) \times (0,2\uppi) \to \mathcal{D}$ be the [coordinate transformation](../Euclidean%20Space/Coordinate%20Transformations.md) from [spherical coordinates](../Euclidean%20Space/Spherical%20Coordinates.md):
>
>$$\mathcal{T}(r, \theta, \varphi) = \begin{bmatrix}r \sin \theta \cos \varphi \\ r \sin \theta \sin \varphi \\ r \cos \theta\end{bmatrix}$$
>
>The **spherical coordinate representation** of $f$ is the [composition](../../Functions/Composition%20(Functions).md) $f \circ \mathcal{T}$.
