# Local Coordinate Bases

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Local Coordinate Bases
>
>Let $(U, \phi)$ be a [chart](../../../Geometry/Manifolds/Coordinate%20Systems/Charts.md) on the [Euclidean space](./Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$ and let $\tau: \phi(U) \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}^n$ be the [transition map](../../../Geometry/Manifolds/Coordinate%20Systems/Transition%20Maps.md) from $(U, \phi)$ to [Cartesian coordinates](./Cartesian%20Coordinate%20System.md).
>
>If $\tau$ is [totally differentiable](../Real%20Vector%20Functions/Differentiation/Total%20Differentiability%20(Real%20Vector%20Functions).md) at $\phi(\boldsymbol{p})$ and the [determinant](../../../Algebra/Matrices/Square%20Matrices/Determinants.md) of its [Jacobian matrix](../Real%20Vector%20Functions/Differentiation/Jacobian%20Matrix.md) $J_{\tau}(\phi(\boldsymbol{p}))$ is non-zero, then the columns of $J_{\tau}(\phi(\boldsymbol{p}))$ form a [Hamel basis](../../../Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Hamel%20Bases.md) for $\mathbb{R}^n$.
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Local Coordinate Basis
>>
>>This [Hamel basis](../../../Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Hamel%20Bases.md)  is known as the **local coordinate basis** of $\phi$ at $\boldsymbol{p}$.
