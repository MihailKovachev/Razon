# Subsequence

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Subsequence
>
>Let $(x_n)_{n \in \mathcal{I}}$ be a [sequence](./Sequences.md) of elements of some [set](../../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) $X$.
>
>A **subsequence** of $(x_n)_{n \in \mathcal{I}}$ is another [sequence](./Sequences.md) $(x_n')_{n \in \mathcal{I}'}$ for which there exists a [strictly increasing](../../Real%20Analysis/Real%20Functions/Monotonicity%20of%20Real%20Functions.md) [function](../../Real%20Analysis/Real%20Functions/Real%20Functions.md) $f: \mathcal{I}' \to \mathcal{I}_'$ such that $s_m = x_{f(m)}$.
>
