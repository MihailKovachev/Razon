# Sequential Compactness

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Sequential Compactness
>
>A [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) $X$ is **sequentially compact** if every [infinite sequence](../Analysis/Functional%20Analysis/Sequences/Sequences.md) $(x_n)_{n \in \mathcal{I}}$ of points in $X$ has a [subsequence](../Analysis/Functional%20Analysis/Sequences/Subsequence.md) which [converges](../Analysis/Functional%20Analysis/Sequences/Convergence%20(Sequences).md) to a [limit](../Analysis/Functional%20Analysis/Sequences/Limits%20(Sequences).md) in $X$.
>
