# Cuts

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Cut
>
>A **cut** of a [directed multigraph](./Directed%20Multigraphs.md)  $G = (V, E, s, t)$ is a [partition](../../Set%20Theory/Partitions.md) of $V$ into two [subsets](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md#Subsets) $S$ and $T$.
