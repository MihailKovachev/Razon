# Vector Space Automorphisms

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Vector Space Automorphism
>
>A **vector space automorphism** is a [bijective](../../../Functions/Injections,%20Surjections%20and%20Bijections.md#Bijections) [endomorphism](./Vector%20Space%20Endomorphisms.md).
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Eigentheory of Automorphisms
>
>Let $V$ be a [vector space](../../../../Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Vector%20Spaces.md) over a [field](../../../../Algebra/Fields/Fields.md) $F$ and let $f: V \to V$ be an [automorphism](./Vector%20Space%20Endomorphisms.md).
>
>If $\lambda$ is an [eigenvalue](./Eigentheory.md) of $f$, then $\lambda \ne 0$.
>
>Some $\lambda \in F$ is an [eigenvalue](./Eigentheory.md) of $f$ if and only if $\lambda^{-1}$ is an [eigenvalue](./Eigentheory.md) of $f^{-1}$. In this case, $\lambda$ and $\lambda^{-1}$ have identical [eigenspaces](./Eigentheory.md), identical [algebraic multiplicities](./Eigentheory.md) and identical [geometric multiplicities](./Eigentheory.md).
