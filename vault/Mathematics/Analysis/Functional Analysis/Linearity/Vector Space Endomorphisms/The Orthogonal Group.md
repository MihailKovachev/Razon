# The Orthogonal Group

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: The Orthogonal Group
>
>Let $(V, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle)$ be a [finite-dimensional](../../../../Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Hamel%20Bases.md#Dimension) [real inner product space](../../../../Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Inner%20Product%20Spaces/Inner%20Product%20Spaces.md).
>
>The [set](../../../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all [orthogonal transformations](./Orthogonal%20Transformations.md) $f: V \to V$ is a [subgroup](../../../../Algebra/Groups/Subgroups.md) of the [general linear group](../The%20General%20Linear%20Group.md).
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: The Orthogonal Group
>>
>>We call this [subgroup](../../../../Algebra/Groups/Subgroups.md) the **orthogonal group**.
>>
>>>[!NOTATION]
>>>
>>>We denote the [orthogonal group](./Orthogonal%20Transformations.md) from $V$ as $O(V)$. When $V$ is the [real vector space](../../../../Algebra/Linear%20Algebra/Real%20Vectors/Real%20Vectors.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$, we also write $O(n, \mathbb{R})$.
>>>
