# Introduction

# Morphology

## Declension

[[Nouns|Nouns]] in [[./index|Modern Greek]] change their form to indicate their [[TODO|case]] and [[TODO|number]]. [[Nouns|Nouns]] are grouped into **declensions** depending on which set of endings they use. The declension to which a [[Nouns|noun]] belongs is largely determined by its [[TODO|gender]] and [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]], but, sometimes, even this information is insufficient to draw a conclusion and you have to check with a dictionary. It is also useful to classify [[Nouns|nouns]] by the position of the [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|stress]] in their [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]]: *oxytone* on the last [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]], *paroxytone* on the second-to-last [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]] and *proparoxytone* on the third-to-last [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]].

### Masculine Nouns

[[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] ending in *-ος* can be either [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]], [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] or [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]]:
- [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|Oxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] remain such throughout the entire declension.
- [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|Paroxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] also remain such throughout the entire declension.
- Most [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] become [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] in both [[TODO|genitive]] forms and the [[TODO|accusative]] [[TODO|plural]]. However, some nouns, especially compounds, remain [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]] throughout.

[[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] with a [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]] in *-ης* are divided into several subgroups depending on the ending of their [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]]: *-ες*, *-ηδες*, *-ητες*, *-εις*. 
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-ης* that don't add anything before the *-ες* of their [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] can be either [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]] or [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]]. [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|Oxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] remain such throughout. [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|Paroxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] remain such except in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]] where they become [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]].
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-ης* that add *-ηδ-* before the *-ες* of the [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] can be either [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]] or [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]].
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-ης* that add *-ητ-* before the *-ες* of the [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] are always [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]]. They keep the [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|stress]] on the same [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]] thoughout except in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]], where it moves to the *-η-* of the ending.
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-ης* with [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] in *-εις* are always [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] and keep the [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|stress]] on the same [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]] throughout.

[[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] with a [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]] in *-aς* are divided into a few subgroups depending on the ending of their [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]]: *-ες*, *-αδες*, *-ηδες* or *-εíς*:
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-ας* that don't add a [[TODO|syllable]] before the *-ες* of the [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] can be either [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] or [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]]. [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|Paroxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] with two [[TODO|syllables]] remain such except in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]], where they become [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]].  [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|Paroxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] with more than two [[TODO|syllables]] remain such throughout. [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|Proparoxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] become [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]].
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-ας* that add *-αδ-* before the *-ες* of the [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] are always [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]] and become [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] in the [[TODO|plural]] because of the added [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]].
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-ας* that add *-ηδ-* before the *-ες* of the [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] are always [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] and become [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]] in the [[TODO|plural]] because of the added [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]].
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-έας* that have a [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] in *-εíς* are [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] in all [[TODO|singular]] forms and in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]]. In the rest of the [[TODO|plural]] forms, they are [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]]. In the [[TODO|singular]], they decline like all other [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-ας* but in the [[TODO|plural]] the ending replaces the final *-ε* of the stem.

[[TODO|Feminine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-η* are divided into two subgroups depending on the ending of their [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]]: *-ες* or *-εις*.
- [[TODO|Feminine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-η* with a [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] in *-ες* can be either [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]], [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] or [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]]. [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|Oxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] remain such throughout, while [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] become [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]] in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]]. Both of these types have a [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]] in just *-ων*. Some [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]] [[Nouns|nouns]] have a [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]] in just *-ων*, while others in *-εων*. Those with a [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]] in just *-ων* become [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]] in this form, while those with a [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]] in *-εων* remain [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]] (but in doing so move the [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|stress]] one [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]] to the right).
- [[TODO|Feminine]] [[Nouns|nouns]] in *-η* with a [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] in *-εις* can be either [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]] or [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]]. The former keep the [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|stress]] on the same [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]] throughout, while the latter move it one [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]] to the right in all [[TODO|plural]] forms.
