# Normal Vectors (Real Parametric Surfaces)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Normal Vector
>
>Let $\phi: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be a [parametric surface](../Real%20Parametric%20Surfaces.md) which is [totally differentiable](../../Real%20Vector%20Functions/Differentiation/Total%20Differentiability%20(Real%20Vector%20Functions).md) at an [interior point](../../../../Topology/Interior,%20Boundary,%20Exterior.md) $\boldsymbol{p}$ of $\mathcal{D}$.
>
>The **normal vector** of $\phi$ at $\boldsymbol{p}$ is the [cross product](../../../../Algebra/Linear%20Algebra/Real%20Vectors/Cross%20Product.md) of $\phi$'s [partial derivatives](../../Real%20Vector%20Functions/Differentiation/Partial%20Differentiability%20(Real%20Vector%20Functions).md) there:
>
>$$\partial_1 \phi(\boldsymbol{p}) \times \partial_2 \phi(\boldsymbol{p})$$
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Normal Vector and Regularity
>
>Let $\phi: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^3$ be a [parametric surface](../Real%20Parametric%20Surfaces.md) which is [totally differentiable](../../Real%20Vector%20Functions/Differentiation/Total%20Differentiability%20(Real%20Vector%20Functions).md) at an [interior point](../../../../Topology/Interior,%20Boundary,%20Exterior.md) $\boldsymbol{p}$ of $\mathcal{D}$.
>
>Then $\phi$ is [regular](../../Real%20Vector%20Functions/Differentiation/Total%20Differentiability%20(Real%20Vector%20Functions).md) at $\boldsymbol{p}$ if and only if its [normal vector](./Normal%20Vectors%20(Real%20Parametric%20Surfaces).md) there is non-zero:
>
>$$partial_1 \phi(\boldsymbol{p}) \times \partial_2 \phi(\boldsymbol{p}) \ne \boldsymbol{0}$$
