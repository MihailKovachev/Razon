# Closed Ball

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Closed Ball
>
>Let $(M, d)$ be a [metric space](./Metric%20Spaces.md), let $p \in M$ and let $r \gt 0$.
>
>The **closed ball** of radius $r$ centered at $p$ is the [set](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all $x \in M$ whose [distance](./Metric%20Spaces.md) from $p$ is less than or equal to $r$:
>
>$$\{x \in M: d(p, x) \le r\}$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$\overline{B_r(p)} \qquad \overline{B(p; r)}$$
>>
>
