The full dative forms are practically extinct nowadays and are only seen in old texts. Instead, they have been entirely supplanted by a construction using the [preposition](../Modern%20Greek/Prepositions.md) *на* with the full [accusative forms](#Personal%20Pronouns).

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: *на* + Accusitive instead of Dative
>
>Тази отговорност се пада <u>нам</u>. 'This responsibility falls to us.'
>
>Тази отговорност се пада <u>на нас</u>. 'This responsibility falls to us.'
>
><u>Тебе</u> го казвам. 'I am saying it <u>to you</u>.'
>
><u>На теб</u> го казвам. 'I am saying it <u>to you</u>.'
>
>Наградата беше дадена <u>ней</u>. 'The reward was given <u>to her</u>.'
>
>Наградата беше дадена <u>на нея</u>. 'The reward was given <u>to her</u>.'
>

The [short personal pronouns](#Short%20Personal%20Pronouns) are semantically equivalent to the full forms, but function as unstressed verbal [clitics](TODO). As such, they are subject to specific rules regarding word order:

- [Short personal pronouns](#Short%20Personal%20Pronouns) in the dative case must precede those in the accusative.
- A [short personal pronoun](#Short%20Personal%20Pronouns) cannot be the first word in a clause.
- [Short personal pronouns](#Short%20Personal%20Pronouns) cannot come before *да*, *не* or *ще*.
- If the third-person singular auxiliary *е* is used, then [short personal pronouns](#Short%20Personal%20Pronouns) must come directly before it.
- If one of the auxiliaries *съм*, *си*, *сме*, *сте*, *са* is used, then [short personal pronouns](#Short%20Personal%20Pronouns) must come directly after it.

$$\text{[ е ]} \;\longrightarrow\; \mathbf{DAT} \;\longrightarrow\; \mathbf{ACC} \;\longrightarrow\; \text{[ съм, си, сме, сте, са ]}$$

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>|Example|Translation|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|Дал съм <u>му</u> парите.|I have given <u>him</u> the money.|The [short personal pronoun](#Short%20Personal%20Pronouns) *му* must come after the auxiliary *съм*.|
>|Предай <u>ѝ</u> документите си.|Hand your documents over <u>to her</u>.|The [short personal pronoun](#Short%20Personal%20Pronouns) *ѝ* cannot be the first word in the clause, so it must go after the [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) *предай*.|
>
