# Compactness

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Open Cover
>
>Let $(X, \tau)$ be a [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md).
>
>A [cover](../Set%20Theory/Covers.md) of $X$ is an **open cover** if all of its elements are [open sets](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md#Open%20Sets).
>

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Compactness of a Topological Space
>
>A [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) is **compact** if each of its [open covers](./Compactness.md) has a finite [subcover](../Set%20Theory/Covers.md).
>

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Compactness of a Subset
>
>A [subset](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of a [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) is **compact** if it is [compact](./Compactness.md) as a [subspace](./Topological%20Subspaces.md).
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Compactness in Subspace $\iff$ Compactness in Ambient Space
>
>Let $X$ be a [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) and let $S$ be a [topological subspace](./Topological%20Subspaces.md) of $X$.
>
>A [subset](../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $\Sigma \subseteq S$ is [compact](./Compactness.md) in $S$ if and only if it is [compact](./Compactness.md) in $X$.
