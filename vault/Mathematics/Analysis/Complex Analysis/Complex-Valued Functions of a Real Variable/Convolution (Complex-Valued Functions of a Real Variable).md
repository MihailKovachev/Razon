# Convolution (Complex-Valued Functions of a Real Variable)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: (Complex-Valued Functions of a Real Variable)
>
>Let $f: \mathcal{D}_{f} \subseteq \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ and $g: \mathcal{D}_{g} \subseteq \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be [complex-valued functions of a real variable](./Complex-Valued%20Function%20of%20a%20Real%20Variable.md).
>
>$$\int f(\tau) g(t - \tau)\,\mathrm{d}\tau$$
>
