# Immittance Converters

**Immittance converters** are a general class of [electronic devices](../../Electronic%20Circuits.md) capable of transforming the behavior of other [components](../../Electronic%20Circuits.md). If you take an [electronic component](../../Electronic%20Circuits.md) and attach it to one end of an [immittance converter](./Negative%20Immittance%20Converters.md), then at the other end you get something which behaves as a completely different [electronic component](../Electronic%20Circuits.md). They are extremely useful because they allow us to simulate [electronic components](../../Electronic%20Circuits.md) which would otherwise be very difficult or impossible to build.
