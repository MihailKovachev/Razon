# P-Norms

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: P-Norm
>
>For all [real numbers](../../Fields/The%20Real%20Numbers/The%20Real%20Numbers.md) $p \ge 1$, the [function](../../../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Real-Valued%20Functions%20of%20Multiple%20Real%20Variables/Real-Valued%20Functions%20of%20Multiple%20Real%20Variables.md) $||\cdot||_p: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ defined as
>
>$$||\boldsymbol{x}|| = \left( \sum_{k = 1}^n |x_k|^p \right)^{\frac{1}{p}}$$
>
>for all $\begin{bmatrix} x_1 & \cdots & x_n \end{bmatrix}^{\mathsf{T}} \in \mathbb{R}^n$ is a [norm](../../Vector%20Spaces/Norms.md) on the [vector space](./Real%20Vectors.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$.
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: P-Norm
>>
>>We call $||\cdot||_p$ the $p$**-norm** or $l_p$**-norm** on $\mathbb{R}^n$.
