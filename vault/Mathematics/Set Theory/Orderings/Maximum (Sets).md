# Maximum (Sets)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Maximum (Sets)
>
>Let $(X, \le)$ be a [partially ordered set](./Partially%20Ordered%20Set.md) and let $S$ be a [subset](../Subsets.md) of $X$.
>
>The **maximum** of $S$ is the greatest element of $S$ (if it exists):
>
>$$M \in S \text{ such that } x \le M \text{ for all } x \in S$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$\max S$$
>>
