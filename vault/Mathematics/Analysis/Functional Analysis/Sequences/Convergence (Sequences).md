# Convergence (Sequences)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Convergence  (Sequences)
>
>Let $X$ be a [topological space](../../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md).
>
>A [sequence](./Sequences.md) $(x_n)_{n \in \mathcal{I}}$ of points in $X$ is **convergent** if it has at least one [limit](./Limits%20(Sequences).md).
>
