# Polar Coordinate Representations (Real Scalar Fields)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Polar Coordinate Representation
>
>Let $f: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}$ be a [real scalar field](./Real-Valued%20Functions%20of%20Multiple%20Real%20Variables.md) and let $\mathcal{T}: [0, \infty) \times (0, 2\uppi) \subset \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathcal{D}$ be the [coordinate transformation](../Euclidean%20Space/Coordinate%20Transformations.md) from [polar coordinates](../Euclidean%20Space/Polar%20Coordinates.md):
>
>$$\mathcal{T}(\rho, \varphi) = \begin{bmatrix} \rho \cos \varphi \\ \rho \sin \varphi \end{bmatrix}$$
>
>The **polar coordinate representation** of $f$ is the [composition](../../Functions/Composition%20(Functions).md) $f \circ \mathcal{T}$.
>
