# Frobenius Norm

>[!DEFINITION] Definition:
>
>The **Frobenius norm** of a [real matrix](./Real%20Matrices.md) $A \in \mathbb{R}^{m \times n}$ is defined as follows:
>
>$$\sqrt{\sum_{i = 1}^m \sum_{j = 1}^n a_{ij}^{2}}$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$||A|| \qquad ||A||_2$$
