# De Broigle Wavelength

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: de Broigle Wavelength
>
>The **de Broigle wavelength** of a [quantum particle](./Quantum%20Particles.md) is the ratio of [Planck's constant](./Planck's%20Constant.md) $h$ to the magnitude of its [momentum](TODO) $\boldsymbol{p}$:
>
>$$\frac{\lambda}{|\boldsymbol{p}|}$$
>
