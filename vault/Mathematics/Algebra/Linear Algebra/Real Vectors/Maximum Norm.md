# Maximum Norm

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Maximum Norm
>
>The **maximum norm** on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is the [function](../../../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Real-Valued%20Functions%20of%20Multiple%20Real%20Variables/Real-Valued%20Functions%20of%20Multiple%20Real%20Variables.md) $||\cdot||_{\infty}: \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{R}$ defined as
>
>$$||\boldsymbol{x}||_{\infty} = \max_{i \in \{1, \dotsc, n\}}|x_i|$$
>
>for all [vectors](./Real%20Vectors.md) $\boldsymbol{x} \in \mathbb{R}^n$.
>
