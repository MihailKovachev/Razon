# Limits (Complex-Valued Functions of a Real Variable)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Limits (Complex-Valued Functions of a Real Variable)
>
>Let $f: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be a [complex-valued function of a real variable](./Complex-Valued%20Function%20of%20a%20Real%20Variable.md) and let $p \in \mathbb{R}$ be an [accumulation point](../../../Topology/Accumulation%20Points.md) of $\mathcal{D}$.
>
>A **limit** of $f$ **at** $p$ is any $L \in \mathbb{C}$ such that for each $\varepsilon \gt 0$, there exists some $\delta \gt 0$ with
>
>$$0 \lt |x-p| \lt \delta \implies |f(x)-L| \lt \varepsilon$$
>
>for all $x \in \mathcal{D}$.
>
