# Compact Support

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Compact Support
>
>Let $X$ be a [topological space](../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) and let $f: X \to F$ be a [function](../Functions/Functions.md) from $X$ to a [field](../../Algebra/Fields/Fields.md) $F$.
>
>We say that $f$ has **compact support** if its [closed support](./Closed%20Support.md) is [compact](../../Topology/Compactness.md).
>
