# Electronic Circuits

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Electronic Circuit
>
>An **electronic circuit** is a physical configuration of electronic devices which are connected with one another in a specific way.
>

Electronic components are connected to one another by bridging their **terminals** using conducting wires.

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Terminal (Pole)
>
>A **terminal** or **pole** of an electronic component is a place on the component where it can be connected to a conducting wire.
>

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Node
>
>A **node** in an [electronic circuit](./Electronic%20Circuits.md) is a region of uninterrupted [wire](./Wires.md).
