# Open Ball

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Open Ball
>
>Let $(M, d)$ be a [metric space](./Metric%20Spaces.md), let $p \in M$ and let $r \gt 0$.
>
>The **open ball** of radius $r$ centered at $p$ is the [set](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all $x \in M$ whose [distance](./Metric%20Spaces.md) from $p$ is strictly less than $r$:
>
>$$\{x \in M: d(p, x) \lt r\}$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$B_r(p) \qquad B(p; r)$$
>>
>
