The [phonemes](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /b, /d, /g/ are respectively realized as [b], [d̪], [g] after a pause or a [nasal](TODO) [consonant](../../Linguistics/Consonant.md). Furthermore, /d/ is also realized as [d̪] after /l/. In all other cases, they are respectively realized as the [approximants](../../Linguistics/Approximant.md) [β̞], [ð̞], [ɣ̞].

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Allophones of /b/, /d/, /g/
>
>un burro /un buro/ [um 'bu.ro]
>
>caldo /kaldo/ ['kal.d̪o]
>
>tengo /tengo/ ['teŋ.go]
>
>la boca /la boka/ [la 'β̞o.ka]
>
>nada /nada/ ['na.ð̞a]
>
>lago /lago/ ['la.ɣ̞o]
>

The [phonemes](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /p/, /t/, /k/ are realized respectively as [p], [t̪], [k]. They are never [aspirated](TODO).

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Allophones of /p/, /t/, /k/
>
>pan /pan/ [pan]
>
>tu /tu/ [t̪u]
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /n/ is realized:

- as [m] before /p/, /b/, /m/;
- as [ɱ] before /f/;
- as [n̪] before /t/ and /d/;
- as [n] before [vowels](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Vowels.md), before pauses and before /s/, /l/, /r/;
- as [ɲ] before /t͡ʃ/, /ʝ/, /ʎ/;
- as [ŋ] before /k/, /g/, /x/.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Allophones of /n/
>
>un beso [um be.so]
>
>enfermo [ẽɱ.feɾ.mo]
>
>antes ['ˈãn̪.t̪es]
>
>nada ['na.ð̞a]
>
>un chico [uɲ 't͡ʃi.ko], cónyuge [ˈkõɲ.ɟ͡ʝu.xe]
>
>tengo ['teŋ.go], banco [ˈbãŋ.ko].
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /s/ is realized as [z] before [voiced](TODO) [consonants](../../Linguistics/Consonant.md) and as [s] otherwise. In Northern and Central Spain, there is a tendency towards the retracted [z̠] and [s̠]. In non-Castillian varieties, when /s/ is in a [syllable](TODO)-final position, it can be realized as [h] or be silent.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Allophones of /s/
>
>mismo [ˈmiz.mo]
>
>desde [ˈd̪ez.ð̞e]
>
>In non-Castillian varieties:
>
>esto [ˈes.t̪o], [ˈeh.t̪o], [ˈe.t̪o]
>
>más [mas], [mah], [ma]
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) [θ] exists only in Castillian Spanish. It is realized as [ð] before [voiced](TODO) [consonants](../../Linguistics/Consonant.md) and as [θ] otherwise. Orthographically, it is represented by the letter ⟨z⟩ or the letter  ⟨c⟩ provided that the latter is followed by ⟨e⟩ or ⟨i⟩.   

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Allophones of /θ/
>
>caza ['ka.θa] ('hunt')
>
>cerveza /θeɾˈbeθa/ [θeɾˈβ̞e.θa] ('beer')
>
>cinco /ˈθinko/ [ˈθĩŋ.ko] ('fivr')
>
>juzgar [xuðˈɣ̞aɾ]
>

>[!INFO] Info: /l/
>
>The [phoneme](TODO) /θ/ is assimilated based on the following [consonant](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md):
>
>- It is realized as [l̪] ([dental](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md)) before /t/ and /d/: [ˈal̪.t̪o].
>- It is realized as [l] otherwise.
>

>[!INFO] Info: /ʝ/
>
>The [phoneme](TODO) /ʝ/ may be realized as the [affricate](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) [ɟʝ] after a pause, [nasal](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) or [lateral](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md). Otherwise, it is realized as the [approximant](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) [ʝ˕].
>

## Stress

[Spanish](./Spanish.md) has a [stress system](TODO) which obeys the following rules:

- Pollysyllabic words ending in a [vowel](#Vowels) are [stressed](TODO) on the [penult](TODO).
- Pollysyllabic words ending in *-n* or *-s* are [stressed](TODO) on the [penult](TODO).
- Pollysyllabic words ending in a [consonant](#Consonants) other than *-n* or *-s* are [stressed](TODO) on the [ultima](TODO).

In all words which deviate from the aforementioned rules, the [stressed](TODO) [syllable](TODO) is marked with an [acute accent](./Orthography%20(Spanish).md#Diacritics).
