# Path-Connectedness

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Path-Connectedness
>
>A [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) $X$ is **path-connected** if, for all $x, y \in X$, there exists a [continuous](../Analysis/Continuity/Continuity.md) [function](../Analysis/Functions/Functions.md) $f: [0,1] \to X$ from the [closed interval](../Set%20Theory/Orderings/Interval.md) $[0,1]$ of the [Euclidean space](../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Euclidean%20Space/Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}$ to $X$ such that $f(0) = x$ and $f(1) = y$.
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Path-Connectedness $\implies$ Connectedness
>
>If a [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) is [path-connected](./Path-Connectedness.md), then it is also [connected](./Connectedness.md).
