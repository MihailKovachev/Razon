# Univariate Polynomial Ring

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Univariate Polynomial Ring
>
>Let $R$ be a [ring](../Rings/Rings.md).
>
>If $R$ is [commutative](../Rings/Commutative%20Rings.md), then the [set](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all [univariate polynomials](./Univariate%20Polynomials.md) over $R$ forms a [ring](../Rings/Rings.md) with [univariate polynomial addition](./Univariate%20Polynomial%20Addition.md) and [univariate polynomial multiplication](./Univariate%20Polynomial%20Multiplication.md).
