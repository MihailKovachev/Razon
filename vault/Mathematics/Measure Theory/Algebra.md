# Algebra

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Algebra
>
>Let $X$ be a [non-empty](TODO) [set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md).
>
>An **algebra** over $X$ is a [collection](../Set%20Theory/Collections.md) $\mathcal{S}$ of [subsets](../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) of $X$ with the following properties:
>
>    - The [empty set](TODO) $\varnothing$ and $X$ itself are in $\mathcal{S}$.
>
