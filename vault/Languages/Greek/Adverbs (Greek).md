# Adverbs (Greek)

The only [[TODO|grammatical category]] exhibited by [[TODO|adverbs]] in [Modern Greek](./Greek.md) is [[TODO|degree]].

## Positive Degree

The positive [degree](#Degrees) of many [adverbs](./Adverbs%20(Greek).md) is formed from the [positive degree](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md#Positive%20Degree) of a corresponding [adjective](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md).

Most [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md) in *-ος*, *-η* / *-α*, *-ο*, in *-ύς*, *-ιά*, *-ύ* and in *-ης*, *-α*, *-ικο* have a corresponding [adverb](./Adverbs%20(Greek).md), which is equivalent to their [[TODO|neuter]] [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] and thus ends in *-α*.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Adverbs in *-α*
>
>αλύπητος, -η, -ο ("merciless") - αλύπητα ("mercilessly")
>
>τελευταίος, -α, -ο ("recent") - τελευταία ("recently")
>
>βαθύς, -ιά, -ύ ("deep") - βαθιά ("deeply")
>

Some [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md) in *-ος*, *-η* / *-α*, *-ο* have an alternative [adverb](./Adverbs%20(Greek).md) formed by adding the suffix *-ως* to their stem. Such [adverbs](./Adverbs%20(Greek).md) derived from [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md) become [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent). In some situations, there is no difference in meaning between the two variants, but in many others this difference is significant.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Adverbs in *-ως* from Adjectives in *-ος*, *-η* / *-α*, *-ο*
>
>ευχάριστος, -η, -ο ("pleasant") - ευχαρίστως ("gladly", "with pleasure"); ευχάριστα ("pleasantly")
>
>τέλειος, -α, -ο ("perfect", "complete") - τελείως ("completely"); τέλεια ("perfectly")
>

[Adverbs (Greek)](./Adverbs%20(Greek).md) are formed from [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md) in *-ης*, *-ες* by adding the suffix *-ως* to the their stem. The [stress](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) remains on the same [syllable](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Syllabification) as in the [[TODO|masculine]] [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]]. However, the [adverbs](./Adverbs%20(Greek).md) formed from [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md) in *-ώδης* are [oxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent).

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Adverbs from Adjectives in *-ης*, *-ες*
>
>ακριβής, -ές ("exact") - ακριβώς ("exactly")
>
>ουσιώδης, -ες ("essential") - ουσιωδώς ("essentially")
>

[Adverbs (Greek)](./Adverbs%20(Greek).md) are formed from [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md) in *-ύς*, *-εία*, *-ύ* by adding the suffix *-έως* but are formal and rarely occur in spoken language.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Adverbs from Adjectives in *-ύς*, *-εία*, *-ύ*
>
>βραδύς, -εία, -ύ ("slow") - βραδέως ("slowly")
>
