# Closed Upper Half-Space

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Closed Upper Half-Space
>
>Let $n \in \mathbb{N}_{\ge 1}$.
>
>The $n$**-dimensional closed upper half-space** is the following [subspace](../../../Topology/Topological%20Subspaces.md) of the [Euclidean space](./Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$:
>
>$$\left\{ \begin{bmatrix}x_1 \\ \vdots \\ x_n \end{bmatrix} \in \mathbb{R}^n \mid  x_n \ge 0 \right\}$$
>
