# Variance

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Variance
>
>Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \Pr)$ be a [probability space](../Probability%20Space.md) and let $X: \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be a [random variable](./Real%20Random%20Variable.md).
>
>The **variance** of $X$ is its second [central moment](./Central%20Moments.md):
>
>$$\mathbb{E}[(X - \mathbb{E}[X])^2]$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$\mathop{\operatorname{Var}} (X) \qquad \sigma^2 \qquad V(X) \qquad \mathbb{V}(X)$$
>>
>

>[!THEOREM]
>
>Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \Pr)$ be a [probability space](../Probability%20Space.md) and let $X: \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be a [random variable](./Real%20Random%20Variable.md).
>
>The [variance](./Variance.md) of $X$ is the difference between the [expectation value](./Expectation%20Value.md) of $X$'s square and the square of the [expectation value](./Expectation%20Value.md) of $X$:
>
>$$\mathop{\operatorname{Var}} (X) = \mathbb{E}[X^2] - \mathbb{E}[X]^2$$
