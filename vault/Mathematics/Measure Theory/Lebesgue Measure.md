# Lebesgue Measure

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Lebesgue Measure
>
>Let $\lambda^{\ast}$ be the [Lebesgue outer measure](./Lebesgue%20Outer%20Measure.md) on the [Euclidean space](../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Euclidean%20Space/Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$.
>
>The **Lebesgue measure** on $\mathbb{R}^n$ is the [Carathéodory extension](./Carathéodory%20Extension.md) of $\lambda^{\ast}$.
