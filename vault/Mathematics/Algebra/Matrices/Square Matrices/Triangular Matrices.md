# Triangular Matrices

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Upper Triangular Matrix (Right Triangular Matrix)
>
>An **upper** or **right triangular matrix** is a [Square Matrices](./Square%20Matrices.md) which has non-zero entries only on its diagonal and above it.
>
>$$
>\begin{bmatrix}\ast & \ast & \cdots & \ast \\ 0 & \ddots & \ddots & \vdots \\ \vdots & \ddots & \ddots & \ast \\ 0 & \cdots & 0 &  \ast\end{bmatrix}
>$$
>

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Lower Triangular Matrix (Left Triangular Matrix)
>
>A **lower** or **left triangular matrix** is a [Square Matrices](./Square%20Matrices.md) which has non-zero entries only on its diagonal and below it.
>
>$$
>\begin{bmatrix}\ast & 0 & \cdots & 0 \\ \ast & \ddots & \ddots & \vdots \\ \vdots & \ddots & \ddots & 0 \\ \ast & \cdots & \ast &  \ast\end{bmatrix}
>$$
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Determinant
>
>The [determinant](./Determinants.md) of a [triangular matrix](./Triangular%20Matrices.md) is the product of its diagonal entries.
