# Time Shift

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Time Shift
>
>Let $(\mathbb{T}, \mathbb{W}, \mathfrak{B})$ be a [dynamical system](./Dynamical%20Systems.md), let $w: \mathbb{T} \to \mathbb{W}$ be a [trajectory](./Dynamical%20Systems.md), let $\tau \in \mathbb{R}$ and $\mathcal{D}_{\sigma^{\tau}w}$ be the following [set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md):
>
>$$\mathcal{D}_{\sigma^{\tau}w} \overset{\text{def}}{=} \{t \in \mathbb{T} \mid t + \tau \in \mathbb{T}\}$$
>
>The $\tau$**-shift** of $w$ is the [function](../Analysis/Functions/Functions.md) $\sigma^{\tau} w: \mathcal{D}_{\sigma^{\tau}w} \to \mathbb{W}$ defined as follows:
>
>$$(\sigma^{\tau} w)(t) \overset{\text{def}}{=} w(t + \tau)$$
>
