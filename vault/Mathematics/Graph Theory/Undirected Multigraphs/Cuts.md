# Cuts

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Cut
>
>A **cut** of an [undirected multigraph](./Undirected%20Multigraphs.md) $G = (V, E)$ is a [partition](../../Set%20Theory/Partitions.md) $C = \{S, T\}$ of $V$ into two [sets](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) $S$ and $T$.
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Cut-Set
>>
>>The **cut-set** of $C$ is the [set](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all [edges](./Undirected%20Multigraphs.md) connecting a [vertex](./Undirected%20Multigraphs.md) in $S$ and [vertex](./Undirected%20Multigraphs.md) in $T$.
>>
>
