## Gender

There are three [[TODO|grammatical genders]] in Modern Greek - masculine ("αρσενικό"), feminine ("θηλυκό") and neuter ("ουδέτερο"). These are mostly distinct from the notions of "male" and "female". Gender is exhibited by [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md), [articles](./Definite%20Article%20(Greek).md), [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md), [[TODO|participles]], some [pronouns](./Pronouns%20(Greek).md) and some [numerals](./Numerals%20(Greek).md).

## Case

Modern Greek has four [[TODO|cases]] - [[TODO|nominative]] (ονομαστική), [[TODO|genitive]] (γενική), [[TODO|accusative]] (αιτιατική) and [[TODO|vocative]] (κλητική). This [[TODO|grammatical category]] is exhibited by [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md), [articles](./Definite%20Article%20(Greek).md), [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md), [[TODO|participles]], [pronouns](./Pronouns%20(Greek).md) and some [numerals](./Numerals%20(Greek).md).

## Number

There are two [[TODO|numbers]] in Modern Greek - singular (ενικός) and plural (πληθυντικός). This [[TODO|grammatical category]] is exhibited by [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md), [articles](./Definite%20Article%20(Greek).md), [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md), [[TODO|participles]], [pronouns](./Pronouns%20(Greek).md), [numerals](./Numerals%20(Greek).md) and [verbs](./Verbs%20(Greek).md).

## Degree

Modern Greek has four [[TODO|degrees of comparison]] - positive, comparative, relative superlative and absolute superlative. This [[TODO|grammatical category]] is exhibited only by [adjectives](./Adjectives%20(Greek).md) and [adverbs](./Adverbs%20(Greek).md).

## Person

Modern Greek has three [[TODO|persons]] - first, second and third. This [[TODO|grammatical category]] is exhibited by [verbs](./Verbs%20(Greek).md) and [pronouns](./Pronouns%20(Greek).md).
