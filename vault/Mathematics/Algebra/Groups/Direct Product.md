# Direct Product

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Direct Product
>
>Let $(G_1, \cdot_1), \dotsc, (G_n, \cdot_n)$ be [groups](../../../index.md) and let $\mathcal{G}$ be their [Cartesian product](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md#Set%20Operations).
>
>Their **direct product** is $\mathcal{G}$ with the [operation](../../Analysis/Functions/Functions.md) $\cdot: \mathcal{G} \times \mathcal{G} \to \mathcal{G}$ defined in the following way:
>
>$$
>(a_1, \dotsc, a_n) \cdot (b_1, \dotsc, b_n) = (a_1 \cdot_1 b_1, \dotsc, a_n \cdot_n b_n)
>$$
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Direct Product is a Group
>
>The [direct product](./Direct%20Product.md) is itself a [group](../../../index.md).
