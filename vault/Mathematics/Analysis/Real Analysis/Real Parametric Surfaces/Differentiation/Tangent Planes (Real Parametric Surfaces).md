# Tangent Planes (Real Parametric Surfaces)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Tangent Plane
>
>Let $\phi: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^2 \to \mathbb{R}^n$ be a [parametric surface](../Real%20Parametric%20Surfaces.md) which is [regular](../../Real%20Vector%20Functions/Differentiation/Total%20Differentiability%20(Real%20Vector%20Functions).md) at an [interior point](../../../../Topology/Interior,%20Boundary,%20Exterior.md) $\boldsymbol{p}$ of $\mathcal{D}$.
>
>The **tangent plane** of $\phi$ at $\boldsymbol{p}$ is the [span](../../../../Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Span.md) of its [partial derivatives](../../Real%20Vector%20Functions/Differentiation/Partial%20Differentiability%20(Real%20Vector%20Functions).md) there:
>
>$$\operatorname{span}\{\partial_1 \phi(\boldsymbol{p}), \partial_2 \phi(\boldsymbol{p}) \}$$
>
