>[!INFO] Info: /b/, /d/, /g/
>
>The [phonemes](TODO) /b/, /d/, /g/ are realized as the [voiced stops](TODO) [b], [d̪] ([dental](TODO)), [g] only after a pause or a [nasal consonant](TODO). For /d/ specifically, it is also the [voiced stop](TODO) [d̪] after /l/.
>
>>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Voiced Stops
>>
>>un burro /un buro/ [um 'bu.ro]
>>
>>caldo /kaldo/ ['kal.d̪o]
>>
>>tengo /tengo/ ['teŋ.go]
>>
>
>In all other contexts, /b/, /d/, /g/ are realized as the [approximants](TODO) [β̞], [ð̞], [ɣ̞].
>
>>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Voiced Approximants
>>
>>la boca /la boka/ [la 'β̞o.ka]
>>
>>nada /nada/ ['na.ð̞a]
>>
>>lago /lago/ ['la.ɣ̞o]
>>
>

>[!INFO] Info: /p/, /t/, /k/
>
>The [phonemes](TODO) /p/, /t/, /k/ are never [aspirated](TODO) and /t/ is [dental](TODO).
>
>>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /p/, /t/, /k/
>>
>>pan /pan/ [pan]
>>
>>tu /tu/ [t̪u]
>>
>

>[!INFO] Info: /n/
>
>The [phoneme](TODO) /n/ undergoes [place assimilation](TODO) depending on what follows it:
>- It is realized as [m] ([bilabial](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md)) before /p/, /b/, /m/: un beso [um be.so].
>- It is realized as [ɱ] ([labiodental](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md)) before /f/: enfermo [ẽɱ.feɾ.mo].
>- It is realized as [n̪] ([dental](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md)) before /t/ and /d/: antes ['ˈãn̪.t̪es].
>- It is realized as [n] ([alveolar](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md)) before [vowels](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Vowels.md), pauses and /s/, /l/, /r/: nada ['na.ð̞a].
>- It is realized as [ɲ] ([palatal](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md)) before /t͡ʃ/, /ʝ/, /ʎ/: un chico [uɲ 't͡ʃi.ko], cónyuge [ˈkõɲ.ɟ͡ʝu.xe].
>- It is realized as [ŋ] ([velar](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md)) before /k/, /g/, /x/: tengo ['teŋ.go], banco [ˈbãŋ.ko].
>

>[!INFO] Info: /s/
>
>The usual realization of /s/ is just [s]. In Northern and Central Spain, it tends to be more retracted: [s̺].
>
>It is realized as [z] before [voiced consonants](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md).
>
>>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /s/ as [z]
>>
>>mismo [ˈmiz.mo]
>>
>>desde [ˈd̪ez.ð̞e]
>>
>
>It may be realized as [h] or be dropped completely ([∅]) at syllable-final positions in non-Castilian varieties.
>
>>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /s/ as [h] or [∅]
>>
>>esto [ˈes.t̪o], [ˈeh.t̪o], [ˈe.t̪o]
>>
>>más [mas], [mah], [ma]
>>
>

>[!INFO] Info: /f/
>
>The realization of /f/ is the [labiodental](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) [f]. Some Andean and Central American dialects may realize /f/ as the [bilabial](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) [ɸ].
>

>[!INFO] Info: /x/
>
>The realization of /x/ is one of the following:
>- The [uvular](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) [χ] in Castilian Spanish.
>- The [velar](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) [x] in Mexican Spanish and in Latin America.
>

>[!INFO] Info: /θ/ ("Distinción")
>
>The [phoneme](TODO) /θ/ exists only in Castilian Spanish. It is used for "c" when followed by "i" or "e" and also for "z". It is realized as [ð] before [voiced consonants](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) and as [θ] otherwise.
>
>>[!EXAMPLE]- 
>>
>>caza ['ka.θa]
>>
>>juzgar [xuðˈɣ̞aɾ] 
>> 
>

>[!INFO] Info: /l/
>
>The [phoneme](TODO) /θ/ is assimilated based on the following [consonant](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md):
>
>- It is realized as [l̪] ([dental](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md)) before /t/ and /d/: [ˈal̪.t̪o].
>- It is realized as [l] otherwise.
>

>[!INFO] Info: /ʝ/
>
>The [phoneme](TODO) /ʝ/ may be realized as the [affricate](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) [ɟʝ] after a pause, [nasal](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) or [lateral](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md). Otherwise, it is realized as the [approximant](../../Linguistics/Phonetics/Consonants.md) [ʝ˕].
>

## Stress

[Spanish](./index.md) has a [stress system](TODO) which obeys the following rules:
- Pollysyllabic words ending in a [vowel](#Vowels) are [stressed](TODO) on the [penult](TODO).
- Pollysyllabic words ending in "n" or "s" are [stressed](TODO) on the [penult](TODO).
- Pollysyllabic words ending in a [consonant](#Consonants) other than "n" or "s" are [stressed](TODO) on the [ultima](TODO).

In *all* words which do not obey the above rules, the [stressed](TODO) [syllable](TODO) is marked with an [acute accent](#Orthography).
