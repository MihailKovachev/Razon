# Neighborhood Basis

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Neighborhood Basis
>
>Let $X$ be a [topological space](../Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) and let $p \in X$.
>
>A **neighborhood basis** of $X$ at $p$ is a [collection](../../Set%20Theory/Collections.md) $\mathcal{B}_p$ of [neighborhoods](../Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) of $p$ such that, for each [neighborhood](../Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md#Neighborhoods) $N(p)$, there exists some $B \in \mathcal{B}_p$ with $B \subseteq N(p)$.
>
