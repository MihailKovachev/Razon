# Clopen Sets

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Clopen Sets
>
>A [subset](../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $S \subseteq X$ of a [topological space](./Topological%20Space.md) is **clopen** if it is both [closed](./Closed%20Sets.md) and [open](./Open%20Sets.md).
