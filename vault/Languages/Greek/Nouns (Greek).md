# Greek

## Declension

[Nouns (Greek)](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) in [Modern Greek](./Greek.md) change their form to indicate their [[TODO|case]] and [[TODO|number]]. [Nouns (Greek)](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) are grouped into **declensions** depending on which set of endings they use. The declension to which a [noun](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) belongs is largely determined by its [[TODO|gender]] and [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]], but, sometimes, even this information is insufficient to draw a conclusion and you have to check with a dictionary. It is also useful to classify [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) by the position of the [stress](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) in their [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]]: *oxytone* on the last [syllable](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Syllabification), *paroxytone* on the second-to-last [syllable](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Syllabification) and *proparoxytone* on the third-to-last [syllable](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Syllabification).

### Masculine Nouns

[[TODO|Masculine]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) ending in *-ος* can be either [oxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent), [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) or [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent):
- [Oxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) remain such throughout the entire declension.
- [Paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) also remain such throughout the entire declension.
- Most [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) become [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) in both [[TODO|genitive]] forms and the [[TODO|accusative]] [[TODO|plural]]. However, some nouns, especially compounds, remain [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) throughout.

[Paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) remain such except in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]] where they become [oxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent).

- [[TODO|Masculine]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) in *-ης* with [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] in *-εις* are always [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) and keep the [stress](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) on the same [syllable](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Syllabification) throughout.

[[TODO|Masculine]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) with a [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]] in *-aς* are divided into a few subgroups depending on the ending of their [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]]: *-ες*, *-αδες*, *-ηδες* or *-εíς*:
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) in *-ας* that don't add a [[TODO|syllable]] before the *-ες* of the [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] can be either [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) or [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent). [Paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) with two [[TODO|syllables]] remain such except in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]], where they become [oxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent).  [Paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) with more than two [[TODO|syllables]] remain such throughout. [Proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) become [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]].
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) in *-ας* that add *-αδ-* before the *-ες* of the [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] are always [oxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) and become [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) in the [[TODO|plural]] because of the added [syllable](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Syllabification).
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) in *-ας* that add *-ηδ-* before the *-ες* of the [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] are always [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) and become [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) in the [[TODO|plural]] because of the added [syllable](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Syllabification).
- [[TODO|Masculine]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) in *-έας* that have a [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] in *-εíς* are [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) in all [[TODO|singular]] forms and in the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]]. In the rest of the [[TODO|plural]] forms, they are [oxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent). In the [[TODO|singular]], they decline like all other [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) in *-ας* but in the [[TODO|plural]] the ending replaces the final *-ε* of the stem.

Some [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) have a [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|plural]] in just *-ων*, while others in *-εων*. - [[TODO|Feminine]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) in *-η* with a [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] in *-εις* can be either [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) or [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent). ### Neuter Nouns

>[!IMPORTANT] Important: Nominative, Accusative and Vocative of Neuter Nouns
>
>The [[TODO|accusative]] and [[TODO|vocative]] of [[TODO|neuter]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) are always identical to the [[TODO|nominative]] in the respective [[TODO|number]].
>

[[TODO|Neuter]] [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) ending in *-ο* have a [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|singular]] in *-ου*. [Oxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) and [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) of this [declension](#Declension) keep the [stress](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) on the same [syllable](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Syllabification) in all forms. [Proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md), however, may become [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) in the [[TODO|genitive]]. Furthermore, some [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) [nouns](./Nouns%20(Greek).md) can be both [proparoxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) and [paroxytone](./Orthography%20(Greek).md#Accent) in the [[TODO|genitive]].
