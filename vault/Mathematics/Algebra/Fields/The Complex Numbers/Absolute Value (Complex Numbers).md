# Absolute Value (Complex Numbers)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Absolute Value
>
>The **absolute value** of a [complex number](./Complex%20Numbers.md) $z \in \mathbb{C}$ is the [square root](TODO) of the sum of its [real part](./Complex%20Numbers.md) and its [imaginary part](./Complex%20Numbers.md):
>
>$$\sqrt{\Re(z)^2 + \Im(z)^2}$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$|z|$$
>>
>
>>[!NOTE]
>>
>>The [absolute value](./Absolute%20Value%20(Complex%20Numbers).md) is also known as the **modulus** of $z$.
>>
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Absolute Value under Multiplication
>
>If $z$ and $w$ are [complex numbers](./Complex%20Numbers.md), then the [absolute value](./Absolute%20Value%20(Complex%20Numbers).md) of $z \cdot w$ is the product of the [absolute values](./Absolute%20Value%20(Complex%20Numbers).md) of $z$ and $w$:
>
>$$|z \cdot w| = |z| \cdot |w|$$
