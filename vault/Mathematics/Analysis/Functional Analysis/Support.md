# Support

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Support
>
>Let $X$ be a [set](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md), let $(Y,p)$ be a [pointed set](../../Algebra/Pointed%20Sets.md) and let $f: X \to Y$ be a [function](../Functions/Functions.md).
>
>The **support** of $f$ is the [subset](../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) of $X$ on which $f$ is not equal to $p$:
>
>$$\{x \in X: f(x) \ne p\}$$
>
