# Boundary (Topology)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Boundary Point
>
>Let $S \subseteq X$ be a [subset](../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) of a [topological space](./Topological%20Space.md).
>
>We say that $p \in X$ is a **boundary point** of $S$ if every [neighborhood](./Neighborhoods.md) of $p$ [intersects](../../Set%20Theory/Intersections.md) both $S$ and its [complement](../../Set%20Theory/Set%20Difference.md) $X \setminus S$:
>
>$$\forall N(p): N(p) \cap S \ne \varnothing \text{ and } N(p) \cap (X \setminus S) \ne \varnothing$$
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Boundary
>>
>>The **(topological) boundary** of $S$ is the [set](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all its [boundary points](./Boundary%20(Topology).md).
>>
>>>[!NOTATION]
>>>
>>>$$\partial_X S \qquad \partial S \qquad \operatorname{Bd}_X(S) \qquad \operatorname{Bd}(S)$$
>>>
>>
