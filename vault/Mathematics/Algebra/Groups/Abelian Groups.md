# Abelian Groups

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Abelian Group
>
>A [group](./Groups.md) $(G, \cdot)$ is called **abelian**, if its operation is commutative:
>
>$$
>a \cdot b = b \cdot a \qquad \forall a,b \in G
>$$
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Criterion for Abelian Groups
>
>Let $(G, \cdot)$ be a [group](./Groups.md).
>
>If $g^2 = 1$ for all $g \in G$, then $(G, \cdot)$ is [abelian](./Abelian%20Groups.md).
