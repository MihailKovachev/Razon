# Zero Polynomial

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Zero Polynomial
>
>Let $R$ be a [commutative](../Rings/Commutative%20Rings.md) [ring](../Rings/Rings.md).
>
>The **zero polynomial** over $R$ is the [polynomial](./Univariate%20Polynomials.md) $(0, 0, \dotsc)$.
>
