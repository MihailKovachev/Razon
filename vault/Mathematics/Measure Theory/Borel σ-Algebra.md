# Borel σ-Algebra

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Borel σ-Algebra
>
>Let $X$ be a [topological space](../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) and let $\mathcal{O}$ be the [collection](../Set%20Theory/Collections.md) of all [open sets](../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) in $X$.
>
>The **Borel σ-algebra** of $X$ is the [intersection](../Set%20Theory/Intersections.md) of all [σ-algebras](./σ-Algebras.md) on $X$ which [contain](../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $\mathcal{O}$:
>
>$$\bigcap \{\Sigma: \Sigma \text{ is a }σ\text{-algebra on } X \text{ and } \mathcal{O} \subseteq \Sigma\}$$
>

>>
