# Probability Measure

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Probability Measure
>
>Let $(X, \Sigma)$ be a [measurable space](../Measure%20Theory/Measurable%20Space.md).
>
>A **probability measure** on $(X, \Sigma)$ is a [finite measure](../Measure%20Theory/Finite%20Measure.md) with [total mass](../Measure%20Theory/Measure%20Theory.md) $1$.
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>[Probability measures](./Probability%20Measure.md) are usually denoted by $P$ or $\Pr$.
>>
