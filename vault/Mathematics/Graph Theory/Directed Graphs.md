>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Directed Graph (Digraph)
>
>A **directed graph** / **digraph** $(V, E)$ consists of a [set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) $V$ and a [collection](../Set%20Theory/Collections.md) $E$ of [ordered pairs](../Set%20Theory/Tuples.md) of elements of $V$:
>
>- The elements of $V$ are called **vertices** or **nodes**;
>- The elements of $E$ are called **edges** or **branches**.
>
