# Real Matrices

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Real Matrix
>
>A **real matrix** is a [matrix](../Matrices.md) over the [real numbers](../../Fields/The%20Real%20Numbers/The%20Real%20Numbers.md).
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Determinants and the Cross Product
>
>The [determinant](../Square%20Matrices/Determinants.md) of a [real](./Real%20Matrices.md) [square matrix](../Square%20Matrices/Square%20Matrices.md) $A \in \mathbb{R}^3$ with
>
>$$A = \begin{bmatrix}\vert & \vert & \vert \\ \boldsymbol{a}_1 & \boldsymbol{a}_2 & \boldsymbol{a}_3 \\ \vert & \vert & \vert\end{bmatrix}$$
>
>can be calculated using the [dot product](../../Linear%20Algebra/Real%20Vectors/Dot%20Product.md) and the [cross product](../../Linear%20Algebra/Real%20Vectors/Cross%20Product.md) as follows:
>
>$$\det A = \boldsymbol{a}_1 \cdot (\boldsymbol{a}_2 \times \boldsymbol{a}_3)$$
>
