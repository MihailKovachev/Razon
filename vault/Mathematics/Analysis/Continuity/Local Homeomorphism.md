# Local Homeomorphism

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Local Homeomorphism
>
>Let $X$ and $Y$ be [topological spaces](../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md).
>
>A [function](../Functions/Functions.md) $f: X \to Y$ is a **local homeomorphism** if each $x \in X$ has an [open](../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) [neighborhood](../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) $N$ with an [open](../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) [image](../Functions/Functions.md) $f(N)$ such that the [restriction](TODO) $f\big|_N: N \to Y$ is a [homeomorphism](./Homeomorphism.md) between the [subspaces](../../Topology/Topological%20Subspaces.md) $N$ and $f(N)$.
>

>
>If $f: X \to Y$ is a [homeomorphism](./Homeomorphism.md) between $X$ and $Y$, then $f$ is also a [local homeomorphism](./Local%20Homeomorphism.md) from $X$ to $Y$.
