# Locally Lebesgue-Integrable Functions

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Locally Integrable Function
>
>Let $\mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^m$ be [Lebesgue-measurable](../../../Measure%20Theory/Lebesgue%20Measure.md), let $f: \mathcal{D} \to \mathbb{R}^n$ be a [real vector function](./Real%20Vector%20Functions.md) which is [measurable](../../../Measure%20Theory/Measurable%20Functions.md) w.r.t. the [Lebesgue σ-algebra](../../../Measure%20Theory/Lebesgue%20Measure.md) on $\mathbb{R}^m$ and the [Borel σ-algebra](../../../Measure%20Theory/Borel%20σ-Algebra.md) of the [Euclidean space](../Euclidean%20Space/Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$ and let $S \subseteq \mathcal{D}$ be [open](../../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md).
>
>We say that $f$ is **locally integrable** on $S$ if the [Lebesgue integral](../Lebesgue%20Integral.md) of its [Euclidean norm](../../../Algebra/Linear%20Algebra/Real%20Vectors/P-Norms.md) is finite on every [compact](../../../Topology/Compactness.md) [subset](../../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $K$ of $S$:
>
>$$\int_K ||f|| \,\mathrm{d}\lambda \lt +\infty$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>The [set](../../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all [functions](../../Functions/Functions.md) $f: S \subseteq \mathbb{R}^m \to \mathbb{R}^n$ which are [locally integrable](./Locally%20Lebesgue-Integrable%20Functions.md) on $S$ is commonly denoted in one of the following ways:
>>
>>$$\mathcal{L}_1^{\text{loc}}(S, \mathbb{R}^n)$$
>>
>
