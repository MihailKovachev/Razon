# Verbs (Spanish)

## Infinitive Forms

Every [verb](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) in [Spanish](./Spanish.md) has an [infinitive form](TODO) which serves as its dictionary form and as a way to categorize all [verbs](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) into **conjugation classes**:

- *-ar* conjugation;
- *-er* conjugation;
- *-ir* conjugation.
