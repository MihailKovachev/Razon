# Cofinite Topology

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Cofinite Topology
>
>Let $X$ be a [set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md).
>
>The [collection](../Set%20Theory/Collections.md) containing the [empty set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) and all [subsets](../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) of $X$ with a [finite](../Set%20Theory/Cardinality.md) [complement](../Set%20Theory/Set%20Difference.md) is a [topology](./Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) on $X$.
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Cofinite Topology
>>
>>This [collection](../Set%20Theory/Collections.md) is known as the **cofinite topology** or **finite complement topology** on $X$.
