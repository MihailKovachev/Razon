# Absolutely Continuous Random Variable

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Absolutely Continuous Random Variable
>
>Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \Pr)$ be a [probability space](../Probability%20Space.md).
>
>A [random variable](./Real%20Random%20Variable.md) $X: \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ is **absolutely continuous** if its [probability distribution](./Probability%20Distribution.md) is [absolutely continuous](../../Measure%20Theory/Absolute%20Continuity%20(Measures).md) with respect to the [Lebesgue measure](../../Measure%20Theory/Lebesgue%20Measure.md) on the [real number line](../../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Real%20Number%20Line.md).
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Absolutely Continuous $\implies$ Probability Density Function
>
>Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \Pr)$ be a [probability space](../Probability%20Space.md) and let $X: \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be a [random variable](./Real%20Random%20Variable.md).
>
>If $X$ is [absolutely continuous](./Absolutely%20Continuous%20Random%20Variable.md), then its [probability distribution](./Probability%20Distribution.md) has [Radon-Nikodym derivatives](../../Measure%20Theory/Radon-Nikodym%20Derivative.md) and each of them is a [probability density function](./Probability%20Density%20Function.md) of $X$.
