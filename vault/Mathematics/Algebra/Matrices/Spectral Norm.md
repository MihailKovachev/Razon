# Spectral Norm

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Spectral Norm
>
>The **spectral norm** on the [real](./Real%20Matrices/Real%20Matrices.md) [square matrices](./Square%20Matrices/Square%20Matrices.md) in $\mathbb{R}^{n \times n}$ is the [norm](../Vector%20Spaces/Norms.md) [induced](./Matrix%20Norms.md) by the $l^2$[-norm](../Linear%20Algebra/Real%20Vectors/P-Norms.md).
>
