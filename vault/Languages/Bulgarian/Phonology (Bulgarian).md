The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /a/ is realized as an [open front unrounded vowel](TODO) [a] in [stressed](#Stress) positions and is raised towards a [near-open central vowel](TODO) [ɐ] in [unstressed](#Stress) positions. Orthographically, it is represented by the letters ⟨а⟩ and ⟨я⟩ with the latter also indicating [palatalization](../../Linguistics/Palatalization.md)

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /a/
>
>анус /'a.nus/ ['a.nus] ('anus')
>
>град /grat/ [grat] ('city')
>
>забавям /za.'ba.vʲam/ [zɐ.'ba.vʲɐm] ('to slow down')
>
>патка /'pat.ka/ ['pat.kɐ] ('duck')
>
>путка /'put.ka/ ['put.kɐ] ('pussy')
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /ə/ is realized as a [mid central vowel](TODO) [ə]. Orthographically, it is most commonly represented by the letter ⟨ъ⟩ but also by ⟨а⟩ or ⟨я⟩ in very specific situations.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /ə/
>
>кръв /krəv/ [krəf] ('blood')
>
>път /pət/ [път] ('road')
>
>ъгъл /'ə.gəl/ ['ə.gəɫ] ('angle')
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /ɔ/ is realized as an [open-mid back rounded vowel](TODO) [ɔ] in [stressed](#Stress) positions and is raised towards a [close-mid back rounded vowel](TODO) [o] in [unstressed](#Stress) positions.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /ɔ/
>
>изток /'iz.tɔk/ ['is.tok] ('East')
>
>обирам /ɔ.'bi.ram/ [o.'bi.rɐm] ('to rob')
>
>порно /'pɔr.nɔ/ ['pɔr.no] ('porn')
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /u/ is realized as a [close-mid back rounded vowel](TODO) [u].

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /u/
>
>боклук /bɔk.'luk/ [bok.'luk] ('trash')
>
>укор /'u.kɔr/ ['u.kor] ('reproach')
>
>урок /u.'rɔk/ [u.'rɔk] ('lesson')
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /ɛ/ is realized as an [open-mid front unrounded vowel](TODO) [ɛ]. 

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /ɛ/
>
>перде /pɛr.'dɛ/ [pɛr.'dɛ] ('curtain')
>
>псе /psɛ/ [psɛ] ('dog')
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /i/ is realized as a [close front unrounded vowel](TODO) [i].

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /i/
>
>минута /mi.'nu.ta/ [mi.'nu.tɐ] ('minute')
>
>ритам /'ri.tam/ ['ri.tɐm] ('to kick')
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /j/ is realized as the [voiced palatal approximant](TODO) [j]. Orthographically, it is most commonly represented by ⟨й⟩. When preceded by a [vowel](../../Linguistics/Vowel.md), the letters ⟨ю⟩ and ⟨я⟩ also represent /j/ as part of the combinations /ju/ and /ja/, respectively.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /j/
>
>басейн /ba.'sɛjn/ [bɐ.'sɛjn] ('pool')
>
>броя /brɔ.'ja/ [bro.'jə] ('to count')
>
>воювам /vɔ.'ju.vam/ [vo.'ju.vɐm] ('to war')
>
>Йемен /'jɛ.mɛn/ ['jɛ.mɛn] ('Yemen')
>
>йон /jɔn/ [jɔn] ('ion')
>

The [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /l/ is realized as a [voiced alveolar lateral approximant](TODO) [l] before the [phonemes](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) /ɛ/ and /i/ and as a [velarized](TODO) [voiced alveolar lateral approximant](TODO) [ɫ̪] in all other situations. There is also an increasing tendency to realize the [voiced labial–velar approximant](TODO) [w] instead of [ɫ].

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: /l/
>
>лесен /'lɛ.sɛn/ ['lɛ.sɛn] ('easy')
>
>лимон /li.'mɔn/ [li.'mɔn] ('lemon')
>
>болка /'bɔl.ka/ ['bɔɫ.kɐ] ('pain')
>
>лов /lɔv/ [ɫɔf] ('hunt')
>

### Palatalization

[Bulgarian](./Bulgarian.md) has 15 [palatalized](../../Linguistics/Palatalization.md) [phonemes](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) ("soft consonants"): /bʲ/, /pʲ/, /vʲ/, /fʲ/, /mʲ/, /dʲ/, /tʲ/, /zʲ/, /sʲ/, /t͡sʲ/, /nʲ/, /lʲ/, /rʲ/. All of them can only occur in [syllable](TODO)-initial positions. Orthographically, they are represented by the letter of the corresponding non-[palatalized](../../Linguistics/Palatalization.md) [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) followed by ⟨ь⟩, ⟨ю⟩ or ⟨я⟩.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Palatalized Phonemes
>
>пясък /'pʲa.sək/ ['pʲa.sək] ('sand')
>
>льос /lʲɔs/ [lʲɔs] ('loess')
>
>лют /lʲut/ [lʲut] ('spicy')
>
>ляв /lʲav/ [lʲaf] ('left')
>

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Palatalized vs Non-Palatalized Phonemes
>
>бал /bal/ [baɫ] ('prom')
>
>бял /bʲal/ [bʲaɫ] ('white')
>
>пат /pat/ [pat] ('stalemate')
>
>пят /pʲat/ [pat] ([past passive participle](./Participles%20(Bulgarian).md#Past%20Passive%20Participles) of *пея*, 'to sing')
>
>вал /val/ [vaɫ] ('shaft')
>
>вял /vʲal/ [vʲaɫ] ('sluggish')
>
>фон /fɔn/ [fɔn] ('background')
>
>фьон /fʲɔn/ [fʲɔn] ('foehn wind')
>
>марка /'mar.ka/ ['mar.kɐ] ('brand')
>
>мярка /'mʲar.ka/ ['mʲar.kɐ] ('measure')
>
>дал /dal/ [daɫ] ([past active aorist participle](./Participles%20(Bulgarian).md#Past%20Active%20Aorist%20Participles) of *дам*, 'to give')
>
>дял /dʲal/ [dʲaɫ] ('share')
>
>брата /'bra.ta/ ['bra.tɐ] (*брат*, 'brother' with the [short article](./Definite%20Article%20(Bulgarian).md))
>
>братя /'bra.ta/ ['bra.tʲɐ] ('brothers')
>
>лоза /lɔ.'za/ [ɫo.'za] ('grapevine', feminine singular noun)
>
>лозя /lɔ.'zʲa/ [ɫo.'zʲa] ('vineyards', plural of the neuter singular nouns *лозе*)
>
>цар /t͡sar/ [t͡sar] ('tsar')
>
>цяр /t͡sʲar/ [t͡sʲar] ('remedy')
>
>минор /mi'.nɔr/ [mi'.nɔr] ('minor key')
>
>миньор /mi'.nʲɔr/ [mi'.nʲɔr] ('miner')
>
>лук /luk/ [ɫuk] ('onion')
>
>люк /lʲuk/ [lʲuk] ('hatch')
>
>рапа /'ra.pa/ ['ra.pɐ] (*рап*, 'rap' with the [short article](./Definite%20Article%20(Bulgarian).md))
>
>ряпа /'rʲa.pa/ ['rʲa.pɐ] ('turnip')
>

[Palatalization](../../Linguistics/Palatalization.md) is very common in Eastern dialects, to the point where the [palatalized](../../Linguistics/Palatalization.md) [phonemes](../../Linguistics/Phoneme.md) are used even when they should not be. By contrast, Western dialects exhibit an increasing tendency in the opposite direction - an absolute loss of [palatalization](../../Linguistics/Palatalization.md).

### Phonation

With the exception of /x/, [obstruents](TODO) in [Bulgarian](./Bulgarian.md) undergo regular [voicing](TODO) and [devoicing](TODO) in pairs (/p/↔/b/, /f/↔/v/, /t/↔/d/, /t͡s/↔/d͡z/, /s/↔/z/, /ʃ/↔/ʒ/, /t͡ʃ/↔/d͡ʒ/, /k/↔/ɡ/):

- [Voiced](TODO) [obstruents](TODO) are [devoiced](TODO) in word-final positions, unless the next word starts with a [voiced](TODO) [consonant](../../Linguistics/Consonant.md).
- [Voiced](TODO) [obstruents](TODO) are [devoiced](TODO) before [voiceless](TODO) [obstruents](TODO).
- [Voiceless](TODO) [obstruents](TODO) are [voiced](TODO) before [voiced](TODO) [obstruents](TODO). This does not apply to /s/ before /v/.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Obstruent Voicing and Devoicing
>
>боб /bob/ [bɔp] ('bean')
>
>град /grad/ [grat] ('city')
>
>изток /'iz.tok/ ['ist.ok] ('East')
>
>сватба /'svat.ba/ ['svad.bɐ] ('wedding')
>
