# Finite Measure

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Finite Measure
>
>Let $(X, \Sigma)$ be a [measurable space](./Measurable%20Space.md).
>
>A [finite measure](./Finite%20Measure.md) on $(X, \Sigma)$ is a [measure](./Measures.md) $\mu$ whose [total mass](./Measure%20Theory.md) is strictly less than $\infty$:
>
>$$\mu(X) \lt \infty$$
>

>[!THEOREM]
>
>Let $(X, \Sigma)$ be a [measurable space](./Measurable%20Space.md).
>
>If $\mu$ is a [finite measure](./Finite%20Measure.md), then $\mu(S) \lt \infty$ for all $S \in \Sigma$.
