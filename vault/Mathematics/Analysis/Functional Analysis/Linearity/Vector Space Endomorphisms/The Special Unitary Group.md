# The Special Unitary Group

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: The Special Unitary Group
>
>Let $(V, \langle \cdot, \cdot \rangle)$ be a [finite-dimensional](../../../../Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Hamel%20Bases.md#Dimension) [complex inner product space](../../../../Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Inner%20Product%20Spaces/Inner%20Product%20Spaces.md).
>
>The [set](../../../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all [unitary transformations](./Unitary%20Transformations.md) $f: V \to V$ whose [determinant](./Vector%20Space%20Endomorphisms.md#Determinants) is $+1$ form a [subgroup](../../../../Algebra/Groups/Subgroups.md) of the [unitary group](./The%20Unitary%20Group.md).
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: The Special Unitary Group
>>
>>We call this [subgroup](../../../../Algebra/Groups/Subgroups.md) the **special unitary group**.
>>
>>>[!NOTATION]
>>>
>>>We denote the [special unitary group](./The%20Special%20Unitary%20Group.md) on $V$ by $\operatorname{SU}(V)$. When $V$ is the [complex vector space](../../../../Algebra/Linear%20Algebra/Complex%20Vectors/Complex%20Vectors.md) $\mathbb{C}^n$, we also write $\operatorname{SU}(n, \mathbb{C})$.
>>>
