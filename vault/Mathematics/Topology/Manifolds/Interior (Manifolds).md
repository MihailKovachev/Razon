# Interior (Manifolds)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Interior
>
>Let $M$ be a an $n$-[manifold with boundary](./Manifold%20with%20Boundary.md).
>
>We say that $p \in M$ is an **interior point** of $M$ if $p$ has a [neighborhood](../Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) which is [homeomorphic](../../Analysis/Continuity/Homeomorphic%20Spaces.md) to an [open subset](../Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) of the [Euclidean space](../../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Euclidean%20Space/Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$.
>
