# Behavioral Difference Equations

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Behavioral Difference Equation
>
>Let $(\mathbb{T}, \mathbb{W}, \mathfrak{B})$ be a [dynamical system](./Dynamical%20Systems.md).
>
>A **behavioral difference equation representation** of $(\mathbb{T}, \mathbb{W}, \mathfrak{B})$ consists of two integers $I_{\text{min}}, I_{\text{max}} \in \mathbb{Z}$ with $I_{\text{max}} \ge I_{\text{min}}$, a [set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) $\mathbb{E}$ and two [functions](../Analysis/Functions/Functions.md) $f_1, f_2: \mathbb{W}^{I_{\text{max}} - I_{\text{min}}}$
>
