# Intervals (Music)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Interval
>
>The **interval** between two [frequencies](TODO) $f_1$ and $f_2 \lt f_1$ is the [ratio](TODO) of $f_1$ to $f_2$:
>
>$$\frac{f_1}{f_2}$$
>
>>[!EXAMPLE]-
>>
>>The [interval](./Intervals%20(Music).md) between $f_1 = 440 \,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $f_2 = 220 \, \mathrm{Hz}$ is the following:
>>
>>$$\frac{f_1}{f_2} = \frac{440 \,\mathrm{Hz}}{220 \,\mathrm{Hz}} = \frac{2}{1} = 2:1$$
>>
>
>>[!EXAMPLE]-
>>
>>The [interval](./Intervals%20(Music).md) between $f_1 = 880 \,\mathrm{Hz}$ and $f_2 = 528 \, \mathrm{Hz}$ is the following:
>>
>>$$\frac{f_1}{f_2} = \frac{880 \,\mathrm{Hz}}{528 \,\mathrm{Hz}} = \frac{5}{3} = 5:3$$
>>
>

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Unison
>
>A **(perfect) unison** is the [interval](./Intervals%20(Music).md) between two identical [pitches](./Sound.md#Pitch), i.e. the [interval](./Intervals%20(Music).md) with value $1:1$.
>

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Octave
>
>An **(perfect) octave** is the [interval](./Intervals%20(Music).md) between two [pitches](./Sound.md#Pitch) where the frequency of one of the [pitches](./Sound.md#Pitch) is exactly double the frequency of the other, i.e. the [interval](./Intervals%20(Music).md) with value $2:1$.
>
