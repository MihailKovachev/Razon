# σ-Finite Measure

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: σ-Finite Measure
>
>Let $(X, \Sigma)$ be a [measurable space](./Measurable%20Space.md).
>
>A [measure](./Measures.md) $\mu$ on $(X, \Sigma)$ is **σ-finite** if there exists a [countable](../Set%20Theory/Cardinality.md) [collection](../Set%20Theory/Collections.md) $\mathcal{S}$ of [measurable sets](./Measurable%20Space.md) whose [union](../Set%20Theory/Unions.md) $\bigcup \mathcal{S}$ is $X$ and such that $\mu(S) \lt \infty$ for each $S \in \mathcal{S}$:
>
>$$X = \bigcup \mathcal{S}$$
>
>$$\mu(S) \lt \infty \qquad \forall S \in \mathcal{S}$$
>
