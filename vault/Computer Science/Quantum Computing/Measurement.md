# Measurement

**Measurement** is the only non-[linear](../../Mathematics/Analysis/Functional%20Analysis/Linearity/Linearity%20(Functions).md), non-[invertible](../../Mathematics/Analysis/Functions/Injections,%20Surjections%20and%20Bijections.md) operation we can do with a [quantum state](./Quantum%20Information.md). It is a non-deterministic operation which forces [superpositions](./Quantum%20Information.md#Quantum%20Registers) to collapse to randomly collapse into a [pure state](./Quantum%20Information.md#Quantum%20Registers) and the probability with which the [superposition](./Quantum%20Information.md#Quantum%20Registers) may collapse into each [pure state](./Quantum%20Information.md#Quantum%20Registers) is determined by the coefficients of its [basis representation](../../Mathematics/Algebra/Vector%20Spaces/Hamel%20Bases.md) in the chosen [computational basis](./Quantum%20Information.md).
