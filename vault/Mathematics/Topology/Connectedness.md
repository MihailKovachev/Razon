# Connectedness

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Connectedness (Topological Space)
>
>A [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) $X$ is **connected** if it cannot be represented as the [union](../Set%20Theory/Unions.md) of two [disjoint](../Set%20Theory/Intersections.md) [non-empty](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) [open sets](./Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md).
>
>>[!NOTE] Note: Connected Subsets
>>
>>We often say that a [subset](../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $S \subseteq X$ is **connected**. This means that $S$ is [connected](./Connectedness.md) as a [subspace](./Topological%20Subspaces.md).
>>
>
