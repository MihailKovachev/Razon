# Manifold with Boundary

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Manifold with Boundary
>
>A **manifold with boundary** is a [second-countable](../Bases/Second-Countable%20Topological%20Space.md) [Hausdorff space](../Hausdorff%20Space.md) $M$ for which there exists some $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ such that each $p \in M$ has a [neighborhood](../Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) which is [homeomorphic](../../Analysis/Continuity/Homeomorphic%20Spaces.md) to an [open subset](../Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) of the [closed upper half-space](../../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Euclidean%20Space/Closed%20Upper%20Half-Space.md) $\mathbb{H}^n$.
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Interior Point
>>
>>We say that $p \in M$ is an **interior point** of $M$ if it has a [neighborhood](../Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) which is [homeomorphic](../../Analysis/Continuity/Homeomorphic%20Spaces.md) to an [open subset](../Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) of the [Euclidean space](../../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Euclidean%20Space/Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$.
>>
>>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Interior
>>>
>>>The **interior** of $M$ is the [set](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all its [interior points](./Manifold%20with%20Boundary.md).
>>>
>>>>[!NOTATION]
>>>>
>>>>$$\operatorname{int} M$$
>>>>
>>>
>>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Boundary Point
>>
>>We say that $p \in M$ is a **boundary point** of $M$ if it has a [neighborhood](../Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) which is [homeomorphic](../../Analysis/Continuity/Homeomorphic%20Spaces.md) to an [open subset](../Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) of the [closed upper half-space](../../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Euclidean%20Space/Closed%20Upper%20Half-Space.md) $\mathbb{H}^n$ but no [neighborhood](../Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) which is [homeomorphic](../../Analysis/Continuity/Homeomorphic%20Spaces.md) to an [open subset](../Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) the [Euclidean space](../../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Euclidean%20Space/Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$.
>>
>>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Boundary
>>>
>>>The **boundary** of $M$ is the [set](../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all its [boundary points](./Manifold%20with%20Boundary.md).
>>>
>>>>[!NOTATION]
>>>>
>>>>$$\operatorname{int} M$$
>>>>
>>>
>>
