# Subring

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Subring
>
>Let $(R, +, \cdot)$ be a [ring](./Rings.md).
>
>A [subset](../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $S \subseteq R$ forms a **subring** of $R$ if it forms a [ring](./Rings.md) with the [restrictions](../../Analysis/Functions/Restriction%20(Functions).md) of $+: R \times R \to R$ and $\cdot: R \times R \to R$ on $S \times S$.
>
