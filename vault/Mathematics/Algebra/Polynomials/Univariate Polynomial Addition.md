# Univariate Polynomial Addition

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Univariate Polynomial Addition
>
>Let $R$ be a [ring](../Rings/Rings.md) and let $A = (a_0, a_1, \dotsc)$ and $B = (b_0, b_1, \dotsc)$ be [univariate polynomials](./Univariate%20Polynomials.md).
>
>The **sum** $A+B$ is defined as the following [univariate polynomial](./Univariate%20Polynomials.md):
>
>$$A + B \overset{\text{def}}{=} (a_0 + b_0, a_1 + b_1, \dotsc)$$
>
