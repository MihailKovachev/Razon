# Coulomb's Law

**Coulomb's law** is an empirical law describing the [electromagnetic](./Electromagnetism.md) interaction between two [point charges](./Electric%20Charge.md) which are at [rest](../Classical%20Mechanics/Kinematics.md) with respect to a given [reference frame](../Classical%20Mechanics/Reference%20Frames.md). 

>[!OBSERVATION] Observation: Coulomb's Law
>
>Consider a [reference frame](../Classical%20Mechanics/Reference%20Frames.md) and let $q_1$ and $q_2$ be two [point charges](./Electric%20Charge.md) in a vacuum with [positions](../Classical%20Mechanics/Kinematics.md#Position) $\boldsymbol{r}_1$ and $\boldsymbol{r}_2$, respectively.
>
>If $q_1$ and $q_2$ are at [rest](TODO), then [electromagnetism](./Electromagnetism.md) manifests between them as the following pair of [forces](../Classical%20Mechanics/Newtonian%20Formalism.md), known as **electrostatic forces**:
>
>$$\boldsymbol{F}_{\text{on } q_1} = k_e \frac{q_1 q_2}{|\boldsymbol{r}_{2 \to 1}|^2} \boldsymbol{\hat{r}}_{2 \to 1} \qquad \boldsymbol{F}_{\text{on } q_2} = k_e \frac{q_1 q_2}{|\boldsymbol{r}_{1 \to 2}|^2} \boldsymbol{\hat{r}}_{1 \to 2}$$
>
>![Coulomb's Law](./res/Coulomb's%20Law.svg)
>

The constant $k_e$ is known as the **Coulomb constant** and is defined via the [permittivity of free space](TODO) $\varepsilon_0$:

$$k_e = \frac{1}{4 \uppi \varepsilon_0} \approx 8.987\, 551\, 785\, 972(14) \times 10^9 \, \mathrm{N} \cdot \mathrm{m}^2 \cdot \mathrm{C}^{-2}$$
