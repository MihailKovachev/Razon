>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Index Sets
>
>Let $S$ be a [set](Sets.md) .
>
>We say that a [set](Sets.md) $I$ is an **index set** of $S$ if there exists a [surjection](../Analysis/Functions/Injections,%20Surjections%20and%20Bijections.md#Surjections) $f: I \to S$. We call $f$ an **indexing**.
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>We use $s_i$ to denote the element in $s \in S$ such that $f(i) = s$.
>>
>
