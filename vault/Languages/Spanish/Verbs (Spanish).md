# Verbs (Spanish)

## Infinitives

Every [verb](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) in [Spanish](./Spanish.md) has an [infinitive form](TODO) which serves as its dictionary form and as a way to categorize all [verbs](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) into **conjugation classes**:

- *-ar* conjugation;
- *-er* conjugation;
- *-ir* conjugation.

### Present Indicative Forms

The **present indicative forms** are constructed by attaching an appropriate ending to the [present stem](#Present%20Forms).

## Imperative Forms

[Verbs](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) have special **imperative forms** for the second person. In the singular, this form is obtained by dropping *-r* from the [infinitive](#Infinitives), while the plural is formed by replacing *-r* with *-d*.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Imperative Forms
>
>|Verb|Second-person Singular Imperative|Second-person Plural Imperative|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|aprender ('to learn')|aprende|aprended|
>|bailar ('to dance')|baila|bailad|
>|dormir ('to sleep')|dormi|dormid|
>
>

A few [verbs](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) have irregular [imperative forms](#Imperative%20Forms) for the second-person singular.

|Verb|Second-Person Singular Imperative|
|:--:|:--:|
|decir|di|
|hacer|haz|
|ir|ve|
|poner|pon|
|salir|sal|
|ser|sé|
|tener|ten|
|venir|ven|
