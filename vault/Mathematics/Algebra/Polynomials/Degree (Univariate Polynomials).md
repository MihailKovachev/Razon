# Degree (Univariate Polynomials)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Degree (Univariate Polynomial)
>
>Let $R$ be a [commutative](../Rings/Commutative%20Rings.md) [ring](../Rings/Rings.md).
>
>The **degree** of the [zero polynomial](./Zero%20Polynomial.md) over $R$ is $-\infty$.
>
>If $P = (c_0, c_1, \dotsc )$ is a non-[zero](./Zero%20Polynomial.md) [univariate polynomial](./Univariate%20Polynomials.md) over $R$, then its **degree** is the largest $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$ such that $c_n \ne 0$.
>
