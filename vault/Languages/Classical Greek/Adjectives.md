# Introduction

# Morphology

Similar to [nouns](./Nouns.md), adjective also decline for [[TODO|case]] and [[TODO|number]]. However, they also decline for [[TODO|gender]].

## First-and-Second-Declension Adjectives

The first group of adjectives is are called **first and second declension adjectives** because they use the same endings as [nouns](./Nouns.md) belonging to the [first](./Nouns.md#First%20Declension) and [second](./Nouns.md#Second%20Declension) [declensions](./Nouns.md#Declension). There are a few subgroups here depending on exactly which set of endings adjectives take.

Adjectives in *-ος*, *-ᾱ* / *-η*, *-ον* use the regular [second-declension](./Nouns.md#Second%20Declension) endings in the [[TODO|masculine]] and [[TODO|neuter]] and they use the [feminine first-declension](./Nouns.md#First%20Declension) endings for the [[TODO|feminine]]. As with [nouns](./Nouns.md#First%20Declension), a long *-ᾱ-* in the [[TODO|singular]] is retained only after *ε*, *ι* and *ρ* and changes to *-η-* otherwise.

>[!NOTE] Note: Adjectives of Two Terminations (in *-ος*, *-ον*)
>
>The are some adjectives in *-ος*, *-ον* which lack distinct forms for the [[TODO|feminine]]. These decline just like adjectives in *-ος*, *-ᾱ* / *-η*, *-ον* but the [[TODO|feminine]] forms are identical to the [[TODO|masculine]].
>

Adjectives in *-οῦς*, *-ᾶ* / *-ῆ*, *-οῦν* decline mostly like those in [-ος, -ᾱ / -η, -ον](#First%20and%20Second%20Declension%20Adjectives) but have a few different endings in some cases due to contraction. Once again, a long *-ᾱ-* in the [[TODO|singular]] is retained only after *ε*, *ι* and *ρ* and changes to *-η-* in all other situations.

>[!NOTE] Note: Adjectives in (*-ους*, *-ουν*)
>
>There are adjectives in *-ους*, *-ουν* which lack distinct forms for the [[TODO|feminine]]. These are declined exactly like adjectives in *-οῦς*, *-ᾶ* / *-ῆ*, *-οῦν* except that the [[TODO|feminine]] forms are identical to the [[TODO|masculine]] ones. Additionally, the [accent](./Orthography.md#Accent) is not guaranteed to fall on the ultima in this case.
>

There are also adjectives in *-εως*, *-εων* which use the [Attic declension](./Nouns.md#Second%20Declension) endings. These have identical forms for the [[TODO|masculine]] and [[TODO|feminine]].

## Third-Declension Adjectives

Third-declension adjectives use the same endings as [third-declension nouns](./Nouns.md#Third%20Declension). Their stem is obtained by removing the ending from the [[TODO|genitive]] [[TODO|singular]].

Adjectives with a stem ending in *-ον-* have *-ων*, *-ον* as endings in the [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]]. They lack separate forms for [[TODO|masculine]] and [[TODO|feminine]]. The *-ν-* at the end of the stem disappears before the *σ* of the [[TODO|dative]] [[TODO|plural]] ending. The [[TODO|vocative]] [[TODO|singular]] of the [[TODO|masculine]] and [[TODO|feminine]] is just the stem without ending.

Adjectives in *-ης*, *-ες* have a stem ending in *-εσ-*. The *σ* in the stem drops out when declining and the remaining *-ε-* is contracted with the ending which appears to change it:
- *ε* + *-ος* = *-ους*;
- *ε* + *-α* = *-η* / *-ᾱ*; The contraction to *-ᾱ* can only occur when there is *ε* or *ι* preceding the *-εσ-* of the stem.
