# Mood (Bulgarian)

## Imperative Mood

The [imperative mood](TODO) is used to express commands or appeals for action.

In the second person, the [imperative mood](#Imperative%20Mood) is most commonly expressed directly using an [imperative form](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Imperative%20Forms) of a [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md).

There are three ways to form negative imperatives bu Only [imperfective](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Imperfective%20Verbs) [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) can express a negative imperative.
