# Covers

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Cover
>
>Let $X$ be a [set](Sets.md).
>
>A [collection](Collections.md) $\mathcal{C}$ of [subsets](Sets.md) of $X$ is a **cover** of $X$ if $X$ is a [subset](Sets.md) of the [union](Collections.md) of $\mathcal{C}$.
>
>$$
>X \subseteq \bigcup \mathcal{C}
>$$
>

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Subcover
>
>Let $X$ be a [set](Sets.md) and let $\mathcal{C}$ be a [cover](Covers.md) of $X$.
>
>A **subcover** of $\mathcal{C}$ is [subcollection](Collections.md) of $\mathcal{C}$ which is still a [cover](Covers.md) of $X$.
>
