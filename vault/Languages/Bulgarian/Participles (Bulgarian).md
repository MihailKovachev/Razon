# Participles (Bulgarian)

## Present Active Participles

A present active participle is formed by replacing the [thematic vowel](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms) of the [present stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms) of an [imperfective](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aspect) [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) with an appropriate ending.

<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle">Feminine</th>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle">Neuter</th>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle">Plural</th>
</tr>

<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle" rowspan=2><i>-ящ</i>, <i>-ащ</i>; <i>-ещ</i></td>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle" rowspan=2><i>-яща</i>, <i>-аща</i>; <i>-еща</i></td>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle" rowspan=2><i>-ящо</i>, <i>-ащо</i>; <i>-ещо</i></td>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle" rowspan=2><i>-ящи</i>, <i>-ащи</i>; <i>-ещи</i></td>

- The endings *-ещ*, *-еща*, *-ещо*, *-ещи* are used when the [present stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms) is not [stressed](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Stress) on the thematic vowel.
- The endings *-ящ*, *-яща*, *-ящо* and *-ящи* are used when the [present stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms) is [stressed](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Stress) on the thematic vowel and the latter is not preceded by ⟨ж⟩, ⟨ч⟩ or ⟨ш⟩.
- The endings *-ащ*, *-аща*, *-ащо* and *-ащи* are used when the [present stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms) is [stressed](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Stress) on the thematic vowel and the latter is preceded by ⟨ж⟩, ⟨ч⟩ or ⟨ш⟩.

There are no [stress](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Stresd) shifts.

>|държа̀, държѝш ('to hold', imperfective)|държѝ-|държа̀щ|държа̀ща|държа̀що|държа̀щи|
>|пѝша, пѝшеш ('to write', imperfective)|пѝше-|пѝшещ|пѝшеща|пѝшещо|пѝшещи|
>|седя̀, седѝш ('to sit', imperfective)|седѝ-|седя̀щ|седя̀ща|седя̀що|седя̀щи|
>

## Past Active Aorist Participle

A past active imperfect participle is formed from the first-person singular [aorist](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms) by replacing the ending *-х* with one of the following endings:

<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle">Feminine</th>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle">Neuter</th>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle">Plural</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody>
<tr>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle"><i>-л</i></td>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle"><i>-ла</i></td>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle"><i>-ло</i></td>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align: middle"><i>-ли</i></td>
</tr>
</tbody>
</table>

- There are no [stress](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Stress) shifts.
- A [stressed](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Stress) ⟨я⟩ before the *-х* ending turns into ⟨е⟩ in the plural if preceded by a [consonant](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Consonants).
- If the first-person singular [aorist](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms) ends in *-тох* or *-дох*, then the entire *-тох* / *-дох* is dropped.
- If the first-person singular [aorist](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms) ends in *-кох*, *-гох*, *-зох* or *-сох*, then the *-ох* is dropped. In the masculine, ⟨ъ⟩ is inserted before the suffix *-л*. Since this ⟨ъ⟩ is only present in the masculine form, the *-ли* ending of the plural can trigger alternations in a [variable yat](./Yat%20Reflex%20(Bulgarian).md) in the root.

## Past Passive Participles

A past passive participle is formed by attaching an appropriate ending to the [aorist stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms) of a [transitive](TODO) [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md). There are no [stress](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Stress) shifts even in [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) whose [aorist stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms) is allowed to shift the [stress](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Stress) to the aorist thematic vowel for the [aorist forms](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms) (e.g. *върна*, *кажа*, etc.).

The endings *-т*, *-та*, *-то*, *-ти* are used by:

- all [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) with an [aorist stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms) ending in *-на*;
- all [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) with a monosyllabic [aorist stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms) as well as their prefixial derivatives.

The endings *-ен*, *-ена*, *-ено*, *-ени* are used by:

- all [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) of the [second conjugation](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms) with an [aorist stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms) ending in *-и*. This aorist thematic *-и* is dropped when forming [past passive participles](#Past%20Passive%20Participles);

## Gerund

A gerund is formed by attaching the ending *-йки* to the [present stem](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms) of an [imperfective](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aspect) [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md). In [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) of the [second conjugation](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms), the present thematic ⟨и⟩ is replaced by ⟨е⟩. There are no [stress](./Phonology%20(Bulgarian).md#Stress) shifts.
