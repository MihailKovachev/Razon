# Matrix Similarity

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Matrix Similarity
>
>Two [square matrices](./Square%20Matrices.md) $A, B \in F^{n \times n}$ are **similar** if there is an [invertible matrix](./Matrix%20Invertibility.md) $P \in F^{n \times n}$ such that
>
>$$B = P^{-1} A P.$$
>

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Similarity $\implies$ Equal Determinants
>
>If two [square matrices](./Square%20Matrices.md) are [similar](./Matrix%20Similarity.md), then they have the same [determinant](./Determinants.md).
