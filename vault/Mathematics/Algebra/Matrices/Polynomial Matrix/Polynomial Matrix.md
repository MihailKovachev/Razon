# Polynomial Matrix

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Polynomial Matrix
>
>Let $R$ be a [ring](../../Rings/Rings.md) and let $R[\xi]$ be the [univariate polynomials](../../Polynomials/Univariate%20Polynomials.md) over $R$.
>
>A **polynomial matrix** is any [matrix](../Matrices.md) over $R[\xi]$.
>
