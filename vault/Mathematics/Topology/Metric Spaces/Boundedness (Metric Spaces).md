# Boundedness (Metric Spaces)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Boundedness
>
>Let $(M, d)$ be a [metric space](./Metric%20Spaces.md).
>
>A [subset](../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $S \subseteq M$ is **bounded** if there exists an [open ball](./Open%20Ball.md) $B_r(p)$ which [contains](../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $S$.
>
>$$\exists p \in M, r \in \mathbb{R}_{\gt 0}: S \subseteq B_r(p)$$
>
