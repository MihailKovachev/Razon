# Holomorphic Function

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Holomorphic Function
>
>Let $f: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be a [complex function](./Complex%20Functions.md) and let $U \subseteq \mathbb{C}$ be an [open set](../../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) with $U \subseteq \mathcal{D}$.
>
>We say that $f$ is **holomorphic** on $U$ if $f$ is [differentiable](./Differentiability%20(Complex%20Functions).md) on $U$.
