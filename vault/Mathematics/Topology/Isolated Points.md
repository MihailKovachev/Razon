# Isolated Points

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Isolated Point
>
>Let $X$ be a [topological space](./Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) and let $S$ be a [subset](../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) of $X$.
>
>We say that $p \in S$ is an **isolated point** of $S$ if there is a [neighborhood](./Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) of $p$ which contains no points of $S$ other than $p$:
>
>$$\exists N(p): N(p) \cap S = \{p\}$$
>
