# Extended Real Numbers

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Extended Real Numbers
>
>The **extended real numbers** are the [union](../Set%20Theory/Unions.md) $\mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty, +\infty\}$ of the [real numbers](./Fields/The%20Real%20Numbers/The%20Real%20Numbers.md) with the symbols $-\infty$ and $+\infty$ equipped with an **addition**, **multiplication** and **division** which extend the standard ones as follows.
>
>Addition:
>
>    - $x + (-\infty) = (-\infty) + x = -\infty$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$
>    - $x + (+\infty) = (+\infty) + x = +\infty$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$
>    - $(-\infty) + (-\infty) = -\infty$
>    - $(+ \infty) + (+\infty) = +\infty$
>
>Multiplication:
>
>    - $x \cdot (-\infty) = (-\infty) \cdot x = +\infty$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ with $x \lt 0$
>    - $x \cdot (-\infty) = (-\infty) \cdot x = -\infty$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ with $x \gt 0$
>    - $x \cdot (+\infty) = (+\infty) \cdot x = -\infty$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ with $x \lt 0$
>    - $x \cdot (+\infty) = (+\infty) \cdot x = +\infty$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$ with $x \gt 0$
>    - $(-\infty) \cdot (-\infty) = +\infty$
>    - $(-\infty) \cdot (+\infty) = (+\infty) \cdot (-\infty) = -\infty$
>    - $(+\infty) \cdot (+\infty) = +\infty$
>
>Division:
>
>    - $\frac{x}{-\infty} = 0$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$
>    - $\frac{x}{+\infty} = 0$ for all $x \in \mathbb{R}$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$\overline{\mathbb{R}} \qquad [-\infty;+\infty] \qquad \mathbb{R} \cup \{-\infty, +\infty\}$$
>>
>
>>[!WARNING]
>>
>>The additions $(-\infty) + (+\infty)$ and $(+\infty) + (-\infty)$ are undefined.
>>
>>The multiplications $0 \cdot (-\infty)$, $(-\infty) \cdot 0$, $0 \cdot (+\infty)$ and $(+\infty) \cdot 0$ are undefined.
>>
>>The divisions $\frac{-\infty}{-\infty}$, $\frac{-\infty}{+\infty}$, $\frac{+\infty}{-\infty}$, $\frac{+\infty}{+\infty}$ and all divisions by $0$ are undefined.
>>
>
