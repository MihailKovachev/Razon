# Behavioral Differential Equations

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Behavioral Differential Equations
>
>Let $(\mathbb{T}, \mathbb{R}^n, \mathfrak{B})$ be a [dynamical system](./Dynamical%20Systems.md).
>
>A **behavioral differential equation representation** of $(\mathbb{T}, \mathbb{W}, \mathfrak{B})$ is any [system of real ordinary differential equations](../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Differential%20Equations/Ordinary%20Differential%20Equations/System%20of%20Real%20Ordinary%20Differential%20Equations.md) $\boldsymbol{F}(\cdot) = \boldsymbol{0}$ such that $\mathfrak{B}$ is precisely the [set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) of all [weak solutions](../Analysis/Real%20Analysis/Differential%20Equations/Ordinary%20Differential%20Equations/System%20of%20Real%20Ordinary%20Differential%20Equations.md) whose [domain](../Analysis/Functions/Functions.md) is $\mathbb{T}$:
>
>$$\mathfrak{B} = \{\boldsymbol{w} \in (\mathbb{R}^{n})^{\mathbb{T}}: \boldsymbol{w} \text{ is a weak solution of } \boldsymbol{F}(\cdot) = \boldsymbol{0} \text{ on } \mathbb{T}\}$$
>
