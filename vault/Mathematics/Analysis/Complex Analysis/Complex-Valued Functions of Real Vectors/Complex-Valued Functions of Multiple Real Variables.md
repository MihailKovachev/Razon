# Complex-Valued Functions of Multiple Real Variables

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Complex-Valued Function of a Real Variable
>
>A **complex-valued function of a real variable** is a [complex-valued function](../Complex-Valued%20Functions.md) whose [domain](../../Functions/Functions.md) is a [subset](../../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) of a [real vector space](../../../Algebra/Linear%20Algebra/Real%20Vectors/Real%20Vectors.md):
>
>$$f: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^n \to \mathbb{C}$$
>
