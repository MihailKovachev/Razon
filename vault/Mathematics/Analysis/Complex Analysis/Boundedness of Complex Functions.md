>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Boundedness
>
>Let $f: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ be a [complex function](./Complex-Valued%20Functions.md).
>
>We say that $B \gt 0$ is a **bound** of $f$ on $S \subseteq \mathcal{D}$ if
>
>$$|f(z)| \le B \qquad \forall z \in S$$
>
>We say that $f$ is **bounded** on $S$ if it has a [bound](./Boundedness%20of%20Complex%20Functions.md) on $S$.
