# Formation

## Positive Degree

The positive [[Adverbs#Degrees|degree]] of many [[Adverbs|adverbs]] is formed from the [[Adjectives#Positive Degree|positive degree]] of a corresponding [[Adjectives|adjective]].

Most [[Adjectives|adjectives]] in *-ος*, *-η* / *-α*, *-ο*, in *-ύς*, *-ιά*, *-ύ* and in *-ης*, *-α*, *-ικο* have a corresponding [[Adverbs|adverb]], which is equivalent to their [[TODO|neuter]] [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|plural]] and thus ends in *-α*.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Adverbs in *-α*
>
>αλύπητος, -η, -ο ("merciless") - αλύπητα ("mercilessly")
>
>τελευταίος, -α, -ο ("recent") - τελευταία ("recently")
>
>βαθύς, -ιά, -ύ ("deep") - βαθιά ("deeply")
>

Some [[Adjectives|adjectives]] in *-ος*, *-η* / *-α*, *-ο* have an alternative [[Adverbs|adverb]] formed by adding the suffix *-ως* to their stem. Such [[Adverbs|adverbs]] derived from [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|proparoxytone]] [[Adjectives|adjectives]] become [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|paroxytone]]. In some situations, there is no difference in meaning between the two variants, but in many others this difference is significant.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Adverbs in *-ως* from Adjectives in *-ος*, *-η* / *-α*, *-ο*
>
>ευχάριστος, -η, -ο ("pleasant") - ευχαρίστως ("gladly", "with pleasure"); ευχάριστα ("pleasantly")
>
>τέλειος, -α, -ο ("perfect", "complete") - τελείως ("completely"); τέλεια ("perfectly")
>

[[Adverbs|Adverbs]] are formed from [[Adjectives|adjectives]] in *-ης*, *-ες* by adding the suffix *-ως* to the their stem. The [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|stress]] remains on the same [[Orthography and Phonology#Syllabification|syllable]] as in the [[TODO|masculine]] [[TODO|nominative]] [[TODO|singular]]. However, the [[Adverbs|adverbs]] formed from [[Adjectives|adjectives]] in *-ώδης* are [[Orthography and Phonology#Accent|oxytone]].

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Adverbs from Adjectives in *-ης*, *-ες*
>
>ακριβής, -ές ("exact") - ακριβώς ("exactly")
>
>ουσιώδης, -ες ("essential") - ουσιωδώς ("essentially")
>

[[Adverbs|Adverbs]] are formed from [[Adjectives|adjectives]] in *-ύς*, *-εία*, *-ύ* by adding the suffix *-έως* but are formal and rarely occur in spoken language.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Adverbs from Adjectives in *-ύς*, *-εία*, *-ύ*
>
>βραδύς, -εία, -ύ ("slow") - βραδέως ("slowly")
>
