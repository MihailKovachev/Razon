# Verbs (Bulgarian)

Verbs in [Bulgarian](../../Music/index.md) inflect for person, number, aspect, tense and mood.
They have no infinitives. In dictionaries, verbs are listed by their first-person singular present simple form and their [conjugation](#Conjugation).

To obtain the stem of a verb, remove the ending of its conjugation from the third person singular present simple tense.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Verb Stem Extraction
>
>
>
>пера (I wash), пере (he washes) -> пер-
>
>ям (I eat), яде (he eats) -> яд-
>
>вися (I hang), виси (he hangs) -> вис-
>
>карам (I drive), кара (he drives) -> кар-
>

## Aspect

Every [verb](TODO) in [Bulgarian](./Bulgarian.md) has [aspect](TODO) (**вид**) and belongs to one of two categories - **imperfective aspect** (**несвършен вид**) and **perfrective aspect** (**свършен вид**). The vast majority of verbs must thus be learnt in aspectual pairs.

### Imperfective Verbs

Imperfective verbs represent actions which are in progress, habitual or repeatedly occuring.

Most [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) formed via the suffixes ⟨ув⟩, ⟨ов⟩ and ⟨в⟩ without a prefix are imperfective.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Imperfective Verbs with Suffixes ⟨ув⟩, ⟨ов⟩ and ⟨в⟩
>
>|Example|Translation|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|аресту̀вам|to arrest||
>|атаку̀вам|to attack||
>|гладу̀вам|to be hungry||
>|госту̀вам|to be a guest||
>|дѐйствам|to act||
>|клюка̀рствам|to gossip||
>|критику̀вам|to criticize||
>|лову̀вам|to hunt||
>|нощу̀вам|to spend the night||
>|публику̀вам|to make sth public||
>

### Perfective Verbs

Perfective verbs глаголи от свършен вид represent actions which are viewed as a complete whole, without reference to duration or repetitiveness.

## Present Forms

The present first-person singular serves as the dictionary form of a [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md). It is also the only present form which might be completely irregular.

Building the rest of the present forms requires the present stem which can be obtained in any one of the following ways:

- the present third-person singular, which is always identical to the present stem;
- by removing the ending *-ш* from the present second-person singular;
- by removing the ending *-те* from the present second-person plural.

Dictionaries usually list the present second-person singular to show the present stem.

Based on the ending of the present stem, [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) are divided into three groups:

- **first conjugation** with a present stem in *-е*;
- **second conjugation** with a present stem in *-и*;
- **third conjugation** with a present stem in *-а* or *-я*.

- The vast majority of first-conjugation verbs have a first-person singular ending in *-а*. The ending *-я* comes after ⟨а⟩, ⟨ъ⟩, ⟨о⟩, ⟨у⟩, ⟨е⟩, ⟨и⟩, ⟨ю⟩ and ⟨я⟩ as well in a few other verbs. A very small number of [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) use the ending *-м*.
- The thematic vowel is dropped in the first-person singular (when regular) and the third-person plural.
- Verbs with first-person singular in *-ка*, *-га* alternate ⟨к⟩, ⟨г⟩ with ⟨ч⟩, ⟨ж⟩ before the thematic vowel ⟨е⟩.

### Second-Conjugation Verbs

[Verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) of the second conjugation take the following endings in the present forms:

<table>
<caption>Present Endings (Second Conjugation)</caption>
<tr>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle"></th>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle">Singular</th>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle">Plural</th>
</tr>
<tr>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle">First Person</th>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle"><i>-я</i>, <i>-а</i></td>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle"><i>-м</i></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle">Second Person</th>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle"><i>-ш</i></td>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle"><i>-те</i></td>
</tr>
<tr>
<th style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle">Third Person</th>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle"><i>-</i></td>
<td style="text-align:center;vertical-align:middle"><i>-ят</i>, <i>-ат</i></td>
</tr>
</table>

- The endings *-а* (first-person singular) and *-ат* (third-person plural) are used only after ⟨ж⟩, ⟨ч⟩ or ⟨ш⟩.
- The thematic vowel is dropped in the first-person singular (when regular) and in the third-person plural.
