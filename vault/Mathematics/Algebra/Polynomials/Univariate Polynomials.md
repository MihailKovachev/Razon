# Univariate Polynomials

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Univariate Polynomial
>
>Let $R$ be a [commutative](../Rings/Commutative%20Rings.md) [ring](../Rings/Rings.md).
>
>A **univariate polynomial** $P$ over $R$ is an [infinite sequence](../../Analysis/Functional%20Analysis/Sequences/Sequences.md) $(c_k)_{k \in \mathbb{N}_0}$ of elements of $R$ for which there exists some $N \in \mathbb{N}_0$ with $c_k = 0$ for all $k \ge N$.
>
