# Tail Sequences

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Tail Sequence
>
>Let $X$ be a [set](../../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) and let $(x_n)_{n \in \mathcal{I}}$ be a [sequence](./Sequences.md) of elements $X$.
>
>A **tail sequence** of $(x_n)_{n \in \mathcal{I}}$ is any [sequence](./Sequences.md) $(x_n')_{n \in \mathcal{I}'}$ of elements of $X$ for which there exists some $N \in \mathcal{I}$ such that:
>
>- $\mathcal{I}' = \{n \in \mathcal{I}\mid n \ge N\}$;
>- $x_n' = x_n$ for all $n \in \mathcal{I}$ with $n \ge N$.
>
