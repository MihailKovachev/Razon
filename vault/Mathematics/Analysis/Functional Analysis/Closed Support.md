# Closed Support

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Closed Support
>
>Let $X$ be a [topological space](../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) and let $f: X \to F$ be a [function](../Functions/Functions.md) from $X$ to a [field](../../Algebra/Fields/Fields.md) $F$.
>
>The **closed support** of $f$ is the [closure](../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Closure%20(Topology).md) of the [subset](../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) of $X$ on which $f$ is non-zero:
>
>$$\operatorname{cl}_X \{x \in X: f(x) \ne 0_F\}$$
>
