# Open Circuits

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Open Circuit
>
>An **open circuit** is a [one-port](../../../index.md) whose [V-I characteristic](../../../index.md#V-I%20Characterstic) is given by
>
>$$
>\mathcal{F} = \{(U, 0) \mid U \in \mathbb{R}\}
>$$
>
