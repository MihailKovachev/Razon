# Tense (Bulgarian)

[Bulgarian](./Bulgarian.md) has a total of nine [tenses](TODO): three simple and six compound. However, not all [tenses](./Tense%20(Bulgarian).md) exist in all [moods](./Mood%20(Bulgarian).md) and not all [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) can be used in all existing combinations of [tense](./Tense%20(Bulgarian).md) and [mood](./Mood%20(Bulgarian).md).

## Present Tense

The present tense (**сегашно време**) is expressed directly through a [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md)'s [present forms](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms). [Perfective](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aspect) [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) cannot be used in the [present tense](#Present%20Tense) in the [indicative mood](./Mood%20(Bulgarian).md#Indicative%20Mood).

The [present tense](#Present%20Tense) is used to express actions which happen concurrently with the moment of speaking. In these cases, it can often be translated into the [English](../English/English.md) [present continuous tense](TODO).

## Aorist Tense

The aorist tense (**минало свършено време**) is expressed directly through a [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md)'s [aorist forms](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Aorist%20Forms).

The [aorist tense](#Aorist%20Tense) is used to express a single event which has occurred at a definite, identifiable point in the past. In these cases, it often corresponds with [English](../English/English.md)'s [simple past tense](TODO).

## Imperfect Tense

The imperfect tense (**минало несвършено време**) is expressed directly through a [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md)'s [imperfect forms](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Imperfect%20Forms).

It strongly corresponds to [English](../English/English.md)'s [past continuous tense](TODO).

## Future Tense

The future tense (**бъдеще време**) is constructed using the particle *ще* and a [present form](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms) of a [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md).

## Future in the Past Tense

The future in the past tense (**бъдеще време в миналото**) is constructed using an [imperfect form](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Imperfect%20Forms) of the [auxiliary verb](TODO) *ща* + the particle *да* + a [present form](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md#Present%20Forms) of a [verb](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md), where both *щях* and the [verbs](./Verbs%20(Bulgarian).md) agree with the [subject](TODO) in [person](TODO) and [number](TODO).
