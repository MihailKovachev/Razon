# Unit Sphere

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Unit $n$-Sphere
>
>Let $n \in \mathbb{N}_0$.
>
>The **unit** $n$**-sphere** is the [subspace](../../../Topology/Topological%20Subspaces.md) of the [Euclidean space](./Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}^{n+1}$ defined by the following [subset](../../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md):
>
>$$\{\mathbf{p} \in \mathbb{R}^{n+1} : ||\mathbf{p}|| = 1\}$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$
>>\mathbb{S}^n
>>$$
>>
>
