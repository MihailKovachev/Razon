# Movable Ъ

**Moveable Ъ** (**подвижно ъ**) is a phenomenon where the [phoneme](../../Linguistics/Phonology.md) combinations /rɤ/ and /lɤ/ (⟨ръ⟩ and ⟨лъ⟩) can alternate with /ɤr/ and /ɤl/ (⟨ър⟩ and ⟨ъл⟩), respectively, in various inflections and derivatives of a given word.
