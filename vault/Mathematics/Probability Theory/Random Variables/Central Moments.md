# Central Moments

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Central Moment
>
>Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \Pr)$ be a [probability space](../Probability%20Space.md), let $X: \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be a [real random variable](./Real%20Random%20Variable.md) and let $n \in \mathbb{N}_{\gt 0}$.
>
>The $n$**-th central moment** of $X$ is its $n$-[moment](./Moments.md) about its [expectation value](./Expectation%20Value.md):
>
>$$\mathbb{E}[(X-\mathbb{E}[X])^n]$$
>
