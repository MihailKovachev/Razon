All hundres ending in *-κόσια* are declined like the [[TODO|plural]] of [[Adjectives|adjectives]] in *-ος*, *-α* / *-η*, *-ο*.

Constructing the numbers until 2000 is done by just listing their components, very similar to English. However, the word "and" is not used in Greek and there are a few peculiarities:
- When 2 occurs as a component in a number, it is always the stressed form "δύο".
- When 100 occurs as a component in a number, it is always the full form "εκτόν".
- When declinable numerals occur as components, they are still declined.
- After 1999, the word "χιλίαδες" is used and the numeral accompanying it must be declined for the feminine.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Composite Cardinal Numerals
>
>528 λουλούδια - πεντακόσια είκοσι οκτώ λουλούδια
>
>521 λουλουδιού - πεντακόσιων είκοσι ένος λουλουδιών
>
>2154 νομισμάτων - δύο χιλίαδες εκτόν πενήντα τεσσαρών νομισμάτων
>
