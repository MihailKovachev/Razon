# Univariate Indeterminate

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Univariate Indeterminate
>
>Let $R$ be a [commutative](../Rings/Commutative%20Rings.md) [ring](../Rings/Rings.md).
>
>The **univariate indeterminate** is the [univariate polynomial](./Univariate%20Polynomials.md) $(0,1,0,0,\dotsc)$.
>
