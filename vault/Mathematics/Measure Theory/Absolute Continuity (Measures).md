# Absolute Continuity (Measures)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Absolute Continuity
>
>Let $(X, \Sigma)$ be a [measurable space](./Measurable%20Space.md) and let $\mu, \nu: \Sigma \to [0,\infty]$ be [measures](./Measures.md) $(X, \Sigma)$.
>
>We say that $\mu$ is **absolutely continuous with respect to** $\nu$ if $\mu(S) = 0$ for each $S \in \Sigma$ for which $\nu(S) = 0$. We also say that $\mu$ is **dominated** by $\nu$ or that $\nu$ **dominates** $\mu$.
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$\mu \ll \nu$$
>>
