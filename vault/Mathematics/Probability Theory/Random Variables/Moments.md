# Moments

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Moment
>
>Let $(\Omega, \mathcal{F}, \Pr)$ be a [probability space](../Probability%20Space.md), let $X: \Omega \to \mathbb{R}$ be a [real random variable](./Real%20Random%20Variable.md), let $v \in \mathbb{R}$ and let $n \in \mathbb{N}_{\gt 0}$.
>
>The $n$**-th moment** of $X$ **about** $v$ is the [expectation value](./Expectation%20Value.md) of $(X-v)^n$:
>
>$$\mathbb{E}[(X-v)^n]$$
>
