## Present Progressive Tense

The present progressive tense (**presente progresive**) is formed using a [present form](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md#Present%20Forms) [conjugating](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) of *estar* with a [gerund](./Participles%20(Spanish).md#Gerunds). This construction functions as a single unit: no words are allowed between *estar* and the [gerund](./Participles%20(Spanish).md#Gerund).

The [present progressive tense](#Present%20Progressive%20Tense) is used to express actions taking place at the exact moment of speaking.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>|Example|Translation|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|Juan está duchándose en este momento.|Juan is taking a shower at this moment.||
>

The [present progressive tense](#Present%20Progressive%20Tense) is used to emphasize the temporary nature of a currently ongoing state or action.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>|Example|Translation|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|Él está viviendo con sus padres mientras busca trabajo.|He is living with his parents, while he looks for work.||
>
