# Electric Charge

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Electric Charge
>
>**Electric charge** is a fundamental property of matter which allows it to interact via [electromagnetism](./Electromagnetism.md).
>

[Electric charge](./Electric%20Charge.md) is a [frame](../Classical%20Mechanics/Reference%20Frames.md)-independent [scalar quantity](TODO) which is mathematically described by a [real number](../../Mathematics/Algebra/Fields/The%20Real%20Numbers/The%20Real%20Numbers.md). Its [measurement unit](TODO) in [SI](TODO) is the  **Coulomb** ($\mathrm{C}$).

## Quantization

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Charge Quantization
>
>**Charge quantization** is the observation that the total [electric charge](./Electric%20Charge.md) of a physical object is always an [integer](TODO) multiple of a specific value known as the **elementary charge**.
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Elementary Charge
>>
>>The **elementary charge** is the following constant:
>>
>>$$1.602\,176\,634\times10^{−19}\,\mathrm{C}$$
>>
>>>[!NOTATION]
>>>
>>>$$e$$
>>>
>>
>

This can be largely explained by the fact that the vast majority of matter is comprised of [protons](TODO), [neutrons](TODO) and [electrons](TODO). The [electric charge](./Electric%20Charge.md) of a single [proton](TODO) and a single [electron](TODO) is, respectively, $+e$ and $-e$, while [neutrons](TODO) have no net [electric charge](./Electric%20Charge.md). It is true that there are [particles](TODO) whose [electric charge](./Electric%20Charge.md) is a fraction of the [elementary charge](#Quantization), but they are always bound to other such [particles](TODO) in a way that makes the net charge an [integer](TODO) multiple of $e$.

## Point Charges

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Point Charge
>
>A **point charge** is a model of a physical object with [electric charge](./Electric%20Charge.md) which assumes that all of the object's [electric charge](./Electric%20Charge.md) is concentrated in a single point.
>
