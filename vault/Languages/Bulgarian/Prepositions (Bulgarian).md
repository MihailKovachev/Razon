# Prepositions (Bulgarian)

## *без*

The [preposition](./Prepositions%20(Bulgarian).md) *без* signifies a lack and is mostly equivalent to the English preposition *without*.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>Той остана без пари. ('He was left without money.')
>

The [preposition](./Prepositions%20(Bulgarian).md) *без* is used in expressions of time similar to the English *to*.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>пет без двайсет ('twenty to five')
>
>шест без петнадесет ('fifteen to six')
>

## *в*, *във*

The [preposition](./Prepositions%20(Bulgarian).md) takes the form *във* when the next word starts with *в-* or *ф-* and *в* otherwise.

The [preposition](./Prepositions%20(Bulgarian).md) *в* / *във* signifies an inward movement or an act of entering, similar to the English preposition *into*. This also applies to more abstract contexts.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>Влязоха <u>в стаята</u>. 'They went <u>into the room</u>.'
>
>Колата ще се разбие <u>във фонтана</u>. 'The car is going to crash <u>into the fountain</u>.'
>
>Превърнахте се <u>във вампири</u>. 'You turned <u>into vampires</u>.'
>

The [preposition](./Prepositions%20(Bulgarian).md) *в* / *във* signifies spatial relationships like the English prepositions *in* and *inside*. This also applies to abstract contexts.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>Живея <u>в апартамент</u> на петия етаж. 'I live <u>in a flat</u> on the fifth floor.'
>
>Няма повече пари <u>в сметката</u>. 'There is no money left <u>in the bank account</u>.'
>

The [preposition](./Prepositions%20(Bulgarian).md) *в* / *във* is used with many [nouns](./Nouns%20(Bulgarian).md) to build expressions signifying time.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
><u>В този момент</u> кръвта ми замръзна. 'At this moment, my blood froze.
>
><u>Във вторник</u> има панаир на книга. 'There is a book festival <u>on Tuesday</u>'.
>
>И <u>в този ден</u> ще разбереш какво си изгубил. 'And <u>on this day</u> you will find out what you lost.'
>
>Никой не знае какво се е случило <u>в онази нощ</u>. 'No one knows what happened <u>that night</u>.'
>

## *вместо*

The [preposition](./Prepositions%20(Bulgarian).md) *вмѐсто* signifies the replacing of one thing with another, similar to *instead of* in English.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>Ям банан <u>вместо ябълка</u>. 'I am eating a banana <u>instead of an apple</u>.'
>
><u>Вместо награда</u> получаваш глоба. '<u>Instead of a reward</u>, you get a fine.'
>

## *върху*

The [preposition](./Prepositions%20(Bulgarian).md) *върху̀* signifies motion towards the top of something, similar to English *on* or *on top of*.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>|Example|Translation|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|Той скочи <u>върху масата</u>.|He jumped <u>onto the table</u>.|He was initially somewhere else (e.g. the floor) and jumped on top of the table.|
>

The [preposition](./Prepositions%20(Bulgarian).md) *върху̀* signifies spatial relations similar to the English *on* and *on top of*.

>[!EXAMPLE]-
>
>|Example|Translation|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|Тя седи <u>върху лицето му.</u>|She is sitting <u>on his face</u>.|
>
