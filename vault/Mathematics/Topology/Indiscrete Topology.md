# Indiscrete Topology

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Indiscrete Topology
>
>Let $X$ be a [set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md).
>
>The [collection](../Set%20Theory/Collections.md) $\{\varnothing, X\}$ which contains only the [empty set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) $\varnothing$ and $X$ itself is a [topology](./Topological%20Spaces/Neighborhoods.md) on $X$.
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Indiscrete Topology
>>
>>We call $\{\varnothing, X\}$ the **indiscrete topology** or **trivial topology** on $X$.
