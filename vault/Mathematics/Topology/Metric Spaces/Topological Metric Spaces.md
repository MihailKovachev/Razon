>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Open Sets in the Metric Topology
>
>Let $(M, d, \tau_d)$ be a [topological metric space](./Topological%20Metric%20Spaces.md).
>
>A [subset](../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) $U \subseteq M$ is [open](../Topological%20Spaces/Open%20Sets.md) if and only if for each $p \in U$ there exists an [open ball](./Open%20Ball.md) around $p$ which is [contained](../../Set%20Theory/Subsets.md) in $U$:
>
>$$\forall p \in U: \exists B_{r} (p) \text{ such that } B_r(p) \subseteq U$$
