# Continuity (Complex-Valued Functions of a Real Variable)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Continuity (Complex-Valued Functions of a Real Variable)
>
>Let $f: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R} \to \mathbb{C}$ be a [complex-valued function of a real variable](./Complex-Valued%20Function%20of%20a%20Real%20Variable.md) and let $p \in \mathcal{D}$.
>
>We say that $f$ is **continuous at** $p$ if $p$ is an [isolated point](../../../Topology/Isolated%20Points.md) of $\mathcal{D}$ or if $p$ is an [accumulation point](../../../Topology/Accumulation%20Points.md) of $\mathcal{D}$ and $f(p)$ is equal to the [limit](./Limits%20(Complex-Valued%20Functions%20of%20a%20Real%20Variable).md) of $f$ at $p$:
>
>$$f(p) = \lim_{x \to p} f(x)$$
>
