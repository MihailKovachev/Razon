# Moment of Inertia

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Moment of Inertia
>
>Let $\mathcal{R}(O)$ be a [reference frame](./Reference%20Frames.md), let $l$ be an axis and let $m$ be a [point mass](./Point%20Mass.md) with [position](../Kinematics/Position.md) $\boldsymbol{r}_m$.
>
>The **moment of inertia** of $m$ around $l$ is the product of $m$ and the square of the distance between $\boldsymbol{r}_m$ and $l$:
>
>$$mr^2$$
