# Electric Fields

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Electric Field from Point Charges
>
>Consider a [reference frame](../Classical%20Mechanics/Reference%20Frames.md) and let $q_1, \dotsc, q_n$ be [point charges](./Electric%20Charge.md#Point%20Charges) with [positions](../Classical%20Mechanics/Kinematics.md) $\boldsymbol{r}_1, \dotsc, \boldsymbol{r}_n$, respectively.
>
>The [electric field](./Electric%20Fields.md) at [position](../Classical%20Mechanics/Kinematics.md) $\boldsymbol{r}$ is
>
>$$\boldsymbol{E}(\boldsymbol{r}) = \frac{1}{4 \uppi \varepsilon_0}\sum_{i = 1}^n q_i \frac{\boldsymbol{r} - \boldsymbol{r}_i}{||\boldsymbol{r} - \boldsymbol{r}_i||^3},$$
>
>where $\varepsilon_0$ is the [vacuum permittivity](./Vacuum%20Permittivity.md).
