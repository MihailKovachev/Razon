# Evaluation (Univariate Polynomial)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Evaluation
>
>Let $S$ and $R$ be [commutative](../Rings/Commutative%20Rings.md) [rings](../Rings/Rings.md) such that $S$ is a [subring](../Rings/Subring.md) of $R$ and let $P = c_0 + c_1 X + c_2 X^2 + \cdots + c_n X^n$ be a [univariate polynomial](./Univariate%20Polynomials.md) over $S$.
>
>The **evaluation** of $P$ at $s$ is the following:
>
>$$a_k s^k = a_0 + a_1 s + a_2 s^2 + \cdots + a_n s^n$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$\text{ev}_s P \qquad P(s) \qquad P\vert_s$$
>>
>
