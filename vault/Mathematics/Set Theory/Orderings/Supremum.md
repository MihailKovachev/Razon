# Supremum

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Supremum
>
>Let $(X, \le)$ be a [partially ordered set](./Partially%20Ordered%20Set.md), let $S$ be a [subset](../Subsets.md) of $X$ and let $U$ be the [set](../Sets.md) of all [upper bounds](./Boundedness%20(Sets).md) of $S$.
>
>The **supremum** of $S$ is the [smallest](./Minimum%20(Sets).md) of its [upper bounds](./Boundedness%20(Sets).md) (if it exists).
>
>$$\min U$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$\sup S$$
>>
>
