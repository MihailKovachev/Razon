# Dirac Measure

>[!THEOREM] Theorem: Dirac Measures
>
>Let $(X, \Sigma)$ be a [measurable space](./Measurable%20Space.md) and let $p \in X$.
>
>The [function](../Analysis/Functions/Functions.md) $f: \Sigma \to \overline{\mathbb{R}}$ defined as
>
>$$f(S) \overset{\text{def}}{=} \begin{cases}0, & p \ne S \\ 1, & p \in S\end{cases}$$
>
>for all $S \in \Sigma$ is a [measure](./Measures.md) on $(X, \Sigma)$.
>
>>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Dirac Measure
>>
>>We call $f$ a **Dirac measure** on $(X, \Sigma)$.
>>
>>>[!NOTATION]
>>>
>>>Typically, $f$ is denoted by $\delta_p$. If $p = 0$, then we can write just $\delta$.
>>>
>>
