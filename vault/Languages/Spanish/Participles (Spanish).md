## Gerunds

A [gerund](TODO) is formed by appending an appropriate ending to the [infinitive stem](#Infinitives).

If the [infinitive](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md#Infinitives) ends in a vowel + *-er* / *-ir*, then then the ⟨i⟩ in the *-iendo* ending alternates with ⟨y⟩.

If an *-ir* [verb](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) undergoes a stem vowel alteration in the [present indicative](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md#Present%20Indicative%20Forms), then it experiences the alterarion ⟨e⟩ ~ ⟨i⟩ or ⟨o⟩ ~ ⟨u⟩.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: Gerunds
>
>|Verb|Gerund|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|caer ('to fall')|cayendo|⟨i⟩ ~ ⟨y⟩ alteration due to vowel + *-er*|
>|cambiar ('to change')|cambiando||
>|comer ('to eat')|comiendo||
>|construir ('to build')|construyendo|⟨i⟩ ~ ⟨y⟩ alteration due to vowel + *-ir*|
>|decir ('to say')|diciendo|⟨e⟩ ~ ⟨i⟩ alteration due to ⟨e⟩ ~ ⟨i⟩ alteration in the present indicative|
>|dormir ('to sleep')|durmiendo|⟨o⟩ ~ ⟨u⟩ alteration due to ⟨o⟩ ~ ⟨ue⟩ alteration in the present indicative|
>|leer ('to read')|leyendo|⟨i⟩ ~ ⟨y⟩ alteration due to vowel + *-er*|
>|oír ('to hear')|oyendo|⟨i⟩ ~ ⟨y⟩ alteration due to vowel + *-er*|
>|servir ('to serve')|sirviendo|⟨e⟩ ~ ⟨i⟩ alteration due to ⟨e⟩ ~ ⟨i⟩ alteration in the present indicative|
>|tener ('to have')|teniendo||
>|traer ('to carry')|trayendo|⟨i⟩ ~ ⟨y⟩ alteration due to vowel + *-er*|
>

The following [verbs](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) have irregular [gerunds](#Gerunds).

|Verb|Gerund|
|:--:|:--:|
|ir ('to go')|yendo|
|poder ('to be able to')|pudiendo|

The [gerund](#Gerunds) is used with several [verbs](./Verbs%20(Spanish).md) to form **verbal periphrases** expressing 

- It can be used with *estar* to express actions which are actively taking place at a specific moment in time. This is not done when expressing desires, needs, possessions, knowledge, beliefs or perception.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: *estar* + Gerund
>
>|Example|Translation|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|Juan está duchándose en este momento.|Juan is taking a shower at this moment.||
>|Estaban hablando cuando llegué.|They were talking when I arrived.||
>

- It can be used with *ir* to express actions which are gradually taking place step by step.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: *ir* + Gerund
>
>|Example|Translation|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|Las cosas van mejorando.|Things are getting better.|
>|Los precios van subiendo cada mes.|The prices are rising every month.|
>

- It can be used with *venir* to emphasize continuous accumulation or repetition leading up to a specific present moment.

>[!EXAMPLE]- Example: *venir* + Gerund
>
>|Example|Translation|Notes|
>|:--:|:--:|:--:|
>|Vengo trabajando en este proyecto desde enero.|I have been working on this project since January.|
