# Univariate Polynomial Multiplication

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Univariate Polynomial Multiplication
>
>Let $R$ be a [ring](../Rings/Rings.md) and let $A = (a_0, a_1, \dotsc)$ and $B = (b_0, b_1, \dotsc)$ be [univariate polynomials](./Univariate%20Polynomials.md).
>
>The **product** $A\cdot B$ is defined as the following [univariate polynomial](./Univariate%20Polynomials.md):
>
>$$A \cdot B \overset{\text{def}}{=} (c_0, c_1, \dotsc) \qquad \text{with} \qquad c_n = \sum_{k=0}^n a_k b_{n-k}$$
>
