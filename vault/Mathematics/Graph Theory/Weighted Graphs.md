# Weighted Graphs

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Weighted Graph
>
>A **weighted graph** is a [graph](./Graphs.md) $G = (V, E, \tau, \varphi)$ equipped with a [function](../Analysis/Functions/Functions.md) $w: E \to W$, called the **weight function**, from $E$ to some [set](../Set%20Theory/Sets.md) $W$.
>
>For each $e \in E$, we call $w(e)$ the **weight** of $e$.
>
