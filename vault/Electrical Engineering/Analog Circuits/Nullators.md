# Nullators

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Nullator
>
>The **nullator** is the [one-port](../../index.md) whose [V-I characteristic](../../index.md#V-I%20Characteristic) $\mathcal{F}$ contains only $(0, 0)$
>
>$$
>\mathcal{F} = \{(0,0)\}
>$$
>
