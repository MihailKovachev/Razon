# Infimum

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Infimum
>
>Let $(X, \le)$ be a [partially ordered set](./Partially%20Ordered%20Set.md), let $S$ be a [subset](../Subsets.md) of $X$ and let $L$ be the [set](../Sets.md) of all [lower bounds](./Boundedness%20(Sets).md) of $S$.
>
>The **infimum** of $S$ is the [greatest](./Maximum%20(Sets).md) of its [lower bounds](./Boundedness%20(Sets).md) (if it exists).
>
>$$\max L$$
>
>>[!NOTATION]
>>
>>$$\inf S$$
>>
