# Periodicity (Complex Functions)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Periodicity of Complex Functions
>
>A [complex function](./Complex%20Functions.md) $f: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{C} \to \mathbb{C}$ is **periodic** if there exists some $P \in \mathbb{C}_{\ne 0}$ with
>
>$$f(z + P) = f(z) \qquad$$
>
>for all $z \in \mathcal{D}$.
>
>In this case, $P$ is called a **period** of $f$.
