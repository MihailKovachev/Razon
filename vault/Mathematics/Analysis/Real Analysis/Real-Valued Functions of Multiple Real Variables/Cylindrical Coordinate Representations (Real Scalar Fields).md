# Cylindrical Coordinate Representations (Real Scalar Fields)

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Cylindrical Coordinate Representation
>
>Let $f: \mathcal{D} \subseteq \mathbb{R}^3 \to \mathbb{R}$ be a [real scalar field](./Real-Valued%20Functions%20of%20Multiple%20Real%20Variables.md) and let $\mathcal{T}: (0,+\infty) \times (0,2\uppi) \times \mathbb{R} \to \mathcal{D}$ be the [coordinate transformation](../Euclidean%20Space/Coordinate%20Transformations.md) from [cylindrical coordinates](../Euclidean%20Space/Cylindrical%20Coordinates.md):
>
>$$\mathcal{T}(\rho, \varphi, z) = \begin{bmatrix}\rho \cos \varphi \\ \rho \sin \varphi \\ z\end{bmatrix}$$
>
>The **cylindrical coordinate representation** of $f$ is the [composition](../../Functions/Composition%20(Functions).md) $f \circ \mathcal{T}$.
