>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Open Cuboid
>
>An **open cuboid** in the [Euclidean space](./Euclidean%20Space.md) $\mathbb{R}^n$ is a [subset](../../../Set%20Theory/Sets.md#Subsets) $C$ of $\mathbb{R}^n$ which can be represented using $n$ [open intervals](#The%20Real%20Number%20Line) $I_1, \cdots, I_n$ as
>
>$$
>C = \left\{ \begin{bmatrix}c_1 \\ \vdots \\ c_n \end{bmatrix} : c_1 \in I_1, \dotsc, c_n \in I_n  \right\}
>$$
>
>We call $I_1, \dotsc, I_n$ the **sides** of $C$.
>
