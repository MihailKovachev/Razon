# Orthography (Spanish)

The [Spanish language](./Spanish.md) uses an [alphabet](../../Linguistics/Writing%20Systems.md) with 27 letters and is largely [phonemic](TODO):

|Letter|Spanish Name|Phonemes & Pronunciation Notes|
|:---:|:---:|:---:|
|A a|a|/a/|
|B b|be|/b/|
|C c|ce|/s/ (/θ/ in Castilian Spanish) before &lt;e&gt; and &lt;i&gt;<br>/k/ otherwise|
|D d|de|/d/|
|E e|e|/e/|
|F f|efe|/f/|
|G g|ge|/x/ before &lt;e&gt; and &lt;i&gt;<br>/g/ otherwise|
|H h|hache|/∅/ in all native words<br>/x/ in loanwords|
|I i|i|/i/|
|J j|jota|/x/|
|K k|ka|/k/|
|L l|ele|/l/|
|M m|eme|/m/|
|N n|ene|/n/|
|Ñ ñ|eñe|/ɲ/|
|O o|o|/o/|
|P p|pe|/p/|
|Q q|cu|/k/|
|R r|erre|/ɾ/|
|S s|ese|/s/|
|T t|te|/t/|
|U u|u|/u/|
|V v|uve|/b/|
|W w|uve doble|/w/|
|X x|equis|/x/|
|Y y|ye / i griega|/j/, /ʝ/, /i/|
