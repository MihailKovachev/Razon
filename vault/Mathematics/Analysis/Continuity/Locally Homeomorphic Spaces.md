# Locally Homeomorphic Spaces

>[!DEFINITION] Definition: Locally Homeomorphic Spaces
>
>
>
>A [topological space](../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) $X$ is **locally homeomorphic** to a [topological space](../../Topology/Topological%20Spaces/Topological%20Space.md) $Y$ if there exists a [local homeomorphism](./Local%20Homeomorphism.md) $f: X \to Y$.
>
